# Problem

## Setup

Let \(\mathcal S\) and \(\mathcal A\) be nonempty finite sets with \(|\mathcal S|=S\) and \(|\mathcal A|=A\), and let \(n\) be a positive integer. A policy assigns a probability distribution on \(\mathcal A\) to each context. Contexts have distribution \(\rho\), which need not have full support. A known logging policy \(\pi_0\) satisfies \(\pi_0(a\mid s)>0\) for every \((s,a)\).

For each context-action pair, there is a fixed reward distribution with unknown mean \(r(s,a)\in[0,1]\). The observations are \(n\) i.i.d. triples generated by
\[
s_i\sim\rho,\qquad a_i\sim\pi_0(\cdot\mid s_i),\qquad R_i=r(s_i,a_i)+\xi_i.
\]
Conditional on all sampled context-action pairs, the noises are independent and satisfy, for every \(i\) and \(t\in\mathbb R\),
\[
\mathbb E[\xi_i\mid s_i,a_i]=0,
\qquad
\mathbb E[e^{t\xi_i}\mid s_i,a_i]\leq e^{t^2/2}.
\]
The conditional noise distribution may depend on the context-action pair.

For \(\eta>0\), any finite real-valued array \(u\) on \(\mathcal S\times\mathcal A\), and any policy \(\pi\), define
\[
J(\pi;u)=\sum_{s:\rho(s)>0}\rho(s)\left[\sum_a\pi(a\mid s)u(s,a)-\eta^{-1}\sum_a\pi_0(a\mid s)\log\frac{\pi_0(a\mid s)}{\pi(a\mid s)}\right].
\]
A logarithmic term with \(\pi(a\mid s)=0\) is \(+\infty\), so the corresponding local objective is \(-\infty\). Contexts of zero \(\rho\)-mass do not contribute to \(J\).

Define \(\pi^*(\cdot\mid s)\), for every context \(s\), to be the unique probability distribution maximizing
\[
\sum_a p(a)r(s,a)-\eta^{-1}\sum_a\pi_0(a\mid s)\log\frac{\pi_0(a\mid s)}{p(a)}.
\]
This specifies a maximizer of \(J(\pi;r)\) even at contexts of zero \(\rho\)-mass. Set
\[
\operatorname{SubOpt}(\pi)=J(\pi^*;r)-J(\pi;r),
\qquad
C_*:=\max_{s,a}\frac{\pi^*(a\mid s)}{\pi_0(a\mid s)}.
\]

Given \(\delta\in(0,1)\), let \(N(s,a)\) be the number of samples at \((s,a)\), and let \(\overline r(s,a)\) be their empirical mean, set to \(0\) if \(N(s,a)=0\). Define
\[
b(s,a)=
\begin{cases}
\sqrt{4\log(2SA/\delta)/N(s,a)},&N(s,a)>0,\\
1,&N(s,a)=0,
\end{cases}
\qquad \widehat r(s,a)=\overline r(s,a)-b(s,a).
\]
Let \(\widehat\pi\) be any measurable choice of a maximizer of \(J(\pi;\widehat r)\).

## Task

Prove the following integrated rate characterization.

There are universal constants \(c_\varepsilon,C,c>0\) such that, for every instance in the setup, every \(\eta>0\), every \(\delta\in(0,1)\), and every \(\varepsilon\in(0,c_\varepsilon)\), if
\[
n\geq C\,\eta C_*^2SA\,\frac{\log^2(2SA/\delta)}{\varepsilon},
\]
then \(\operatorname{SubOpt}(\widehat\pi)\leq\varepsilon\) with probability at least \(1-\delta\).

Conversely, suppose \(A\geq2\) is even. Fix \(S,A,\eta,n\). A policy estimator is a measurable rule using the \(n\) observed triples, the known logging policy, and the fixed parameters; it may also use randomness independent of the instance and observations. Suppose it returns a policy with full support at every context. If
\[
n>\max\{16SA,4\eta^2SA\},
\]
then every such estimator \(T\) satisfies
\[
\sup_{\mathcal I}\mathbb E_{\mathcal I}\!\left[\operatorname{SubOpt}_{\mathcal I}\bigl(T(\mathcal D,\pi_0)\bigr)\right]
\geq c\,\frac{\eta SA}{n}.
\]
Here \(\mathcal I\) ranges over all instances in the setup on the fixed context and action sets, including all context distributions, full-support logging policies, and reward distributions satisfying the stated conditions. The expectation includes the data and any estimator randomization; the objective and its maximizer are those of the instance \(\mathcal I\).
