# Problem

## Setup

Conditioning on the observed inputs, let a task `T` have prior `p(T)` and conditional observation law `p_T(y|x)`. For a context `D={(x_i,y_i)}`, assume

`0 < integral p(T) product_i p_T(y_i|x_i) dT < infinity`,

and define its posterior by

`p(T|D) proportional to p(T) product_i p_T(y_i|x_i)`.

For a query input `x_*`, define the posterior predictive by

`p(y_*|x_*,D)=integral p_T(y_*|x_*) p(T|D) dT`.

At any query-context pair for which

`integral E[Y^2|x_*,T] p(T|D) dT < infinity`,

put `m_T(x_*)=E[Y|x_*,T]` and `s_T^2(x_*)=Var(Y|x_*,T)`. The finite predictive variance decomposes as

`Var(Y_*|x_*,D)=Var_{T|D}(m_T(x_*))+E_{T|D}[s_T^2(x_*)]`.

In an augmented synthetic prior, a task contains a clean signal `f_T` and a finite positive noise-variance function `sigma_T^2`. Observations are generated by

`Y=f_T(x)+epsilon`, with `epsilon|x,T ~ N(0,sigma_T^2(x))`.

For each query, the generator supplies the noisy target `Y_*`, clean target `F_*=f_T(x_*)`, and variance label `S_*=sigma_T^2(x_*)` for evaluating training losses. The model's inputs, during both training and testing, are only `(x_*,D)`.

Let `K` be a positive finite integer. Fix finite bin edges `a_0<...<a_K` with centers `c_j=(a_{j-1}+a_j)/2`. Use a fixed endpoint convention that partitions `[a_0,a_K]` into the `K` bins, including the two outer endpoints, and assume `F_*` belongs to this interval almost surely. Let `B(F_*)` in `{1,...,K}` be its containing-bin index.

The model outputs a measurable categorical distribution `pi^(f)(x_*,D)` over these bins and a measurable finite positive variance prediction `sigma_hat^2(x_*,D)`. Categorical distributions may have zero coordinates; use `-log 0=+infinity` in the loss. For fixed finite positive weights `lambda_f,lambda_sigma`, consider the population auxiliary risk

`E[lambda_f(-log pi^(f)_{B(F_*)})+lambda_sigma(log sigma_hat^2-log S_*)^2]`,

where the expectation is over the synthetic task, context, and query experiment. Assume `S_*>0` almost surely, `E[(log S_*)^2]<infinity`, and a model class rich enough to realize every pair consisting of a measurable conditional categorical law and a measurable finite positive variance function of `(x_*,D)`, without a coupling constraint between the two heads. No global finite-predictive-variance assumption is imposed on this auxiliary-risk claim.

## Task

Prove the following integrated claim:

1. For any fixed `(x_*,D)` whose marginal predictive is `N(m,s^2)` with `s^2>0`, the same marginal law is compatible with a continuum of independent additive decompositions `Y_*=F_*+epsilon_*` having latent variance `a` and noise variance `s^2-a`, as `a` ranges over `(0,s^2)`. Hence the marginal predictive alone does not identify an epistemic--aleatoric variance split.

2. Under the augmented synthetic setup, every population minimizer of the stated auxiliary risk satisfies, for almost every `(x_*,D)`,

`pi_j^(f)(x_*,D)=P(B(F_*)=j|x_*,D)` for all `j`,

and

`log sigma_hat^2(x_*,D)=E[log S_*|x_*,D]` (equivalently, `sigma_hat^2=exp(E[log S_*|x_*,D])`).
