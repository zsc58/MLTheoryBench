# Problem

## Setup

Consider branch-and-bound for maximization 0-1 MILPs. At a feasible node \(N\), let \(z(N)\) be its finite attained LP optimum. Choose an optimal node-LP solution using a fixed rule common to both runs, and let the branching candidates be the binary variables fractional in that solution. Branching on a candidate \(j\) creates the two children obtained by fixing that variable to \(0\) and \(1\).

An incumbent is the objective value of a known integer-feasible solution, or \(-\infty\) if no such solution is yet known; it is updated whenever a better integer-feasible solution is found. Nodes are pruned if their LP is infeasible, their chosen LP optimum is integral on the integer coordinates, or their LP bound is at most the incumbent value. Tree size counts every node generated by actual branching, including the root. Node selection is best-bound with a fixed smallest-index tie-break, and branching ties are also resolved by a fixed smallest-index rule.

Fix a score floor \(\rho>0\). For a candidate variable \(j\) at a feasible node \(N\), write \(N_j^0,N_j^1\) for its two child subproblems and define
\[
\Delta_j^b=z(N)-z(N_j^b),\qquad b\in\{0,1\}.
\]
An infeasible child has LP value \(-\infty\), so its improvement is \(+\infty\). The product strong-branching score is
\[
S_{\rm sb}(N,j)=\max\{\Delta_j^0,\rho\}\max\{\Delta_j^1,\rho\};
\]
in particular, this score is \(+\infty\) if either child is infeasible.

Given a scoring rule \(S\), its score-based branching policy selects, at each unpruned node, a candidate maximizing \(S(N,j)\), using the fixed branching tie-break. Write \(\mathcal T_S(Q)\) for the resulting tree on instance \(Q\). The absolute-error condition below requires both compared scores to be finite real numbers.

## Task

Prove that there exists a family \(\{Q_n\}_{n\ge3}\) of maximization 0-1 MILPs, where \(Q_n\) has \(O(n)\) variables, such that for every integer \(n\ge3\) and every \(\varepsilon>0\), there is a scoring rule \(\widehat S\) satisfying the following properties.

At every node occurring in either \(\mathcal T_{S_{\rm sb}}(Q_n)\) or \(\mathcal T_{\widehat S}(Q_n)\), and for every candidate \(j\) there,
\[
|\widehat S(N,j)-S_{\rm sb}(N,j)|\le\varepsilon,
\]
yet
\[
|\mathcal T_{S_{\rm sb}}(Q_n)|\le2n+1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
|\mathcal T_{\widehat S}(Q_n)|\ge2^{n+1}-1.
\]
