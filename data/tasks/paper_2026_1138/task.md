# Problem

## Setup

Fix an integer d>=1, a time horizon T>0, and a batch size b>=1 that remains fixed as eta decreases to zero. Let Theta be an open convex subset of R^d. Let Z_1,Z_2,... take values in a measurable space and have an exchangeable law with a given directing random probability measure mu: conditional on mu, the coordinates are independent with common law P_mu. Write Q for their common unconditional marginal law and E_mu for expectation under P_mu.

Let ell(theta;z) be a measurable loss, twice continuously differentiable in theta, with measurable first and second derivatives. Write psi(theta;z)=grad_theta ell(theta;z). On an open neighborhood U contained in Theta, let the finite population risk be L(theta)=E_Q[ell(theta;Z)], and set

    h(theta)=grad L(theta)=E_Q[psi(theta;Z)],
    H(theta)=D h(theta)=D^2 L(theta).

Let theta_* in U be a local minimizer of L. Assume that there are finite constants M and m_4 such that, for every theta in U,

    ||D_theta^2 ell(theta;Z)||_op <= M  almost surely under Q,
    E_Q[||psi(theta;Z)||^4] <= m_4.

Assume h is continuously differentiable on U and D h is locally Lipschitz there.

For theta in U define

    m_mu(theta)=E_mu[psi(theta;Z)],
    G_mu(theta)=Cov_mu(psi(theta;Z)),
    G(theta)=E[G_mu(theta)].

Assume that, for every theta in U, m_mu(theta)=h(theta) almost surely in mu. Let T_theta=range(H(theta)), let Pi_theta be the Euclidean orthogonal projector onto T_theta, and define

    G*(theta)=Pi_theta G(theta) Pi_theta^T.

Assume G* is continuous on U. The local stability and noise assumptions are: for some constants a>0 and lambda>0,

    v^T H(theta_*)v >= a ||v||^2                  for every v in R^d,
    v^T G*(theta_*)v >= lambda ||v||^2           for every v in T_theta_*.

Let theta_0 be deterministic. Assume the ODE

    d bar theta(t)/dt = -h(bar theta(t)),    bar theta(0)=theta_0,

has a unique solution on [0,T], and that this solution lies in a compact set K contained in U.

A batch B=(Z_1,...,Z_b) has the joint law of the first b coordinates of the exchangeable sequence. For such a batch write

    g_B(theta)=(1/b) sum_{j=1}^b psi(theta;Z_j).

For each eta>0, use independent copies B_1,B_2,... of this batch, independent also of the initial iterate theta_0^eta. Let F_k^eta be the sigma-field generated by theta_0^eta and B_1,...,B_k, and define the recursion

    xi_{k+1}^eta=Pi_{theta_k^eta}(g_{B_{k+1}}(theta_k^eta)-h(theta_k^eta)),
    theta_{k+1}^eta=theta_k^eta-eta[h(theta_k^eta)+xi_{k+1}^eta].

Assume this recursion is well-defined through index ceil(T/eta). Assume there is a finite constant C_K, independent of eta and k, such that for all sufficiently small eta and all integers 0<=k<=floor(T/eta),

    E[||xi_{k+1}^eta||^4 1{theta_k^eta in K} | F_k^eta] <= C_K

almost surely. Assume also

    P(theta_k^eta in K for every integer 0<=k<=floor(T/eta)) -> 1,
    theta_0^eta -> theta_0 in probability,
    U_0^eta=sqrt(b/eta)(theta_0^eta-theta_0) => U_0,

where U_0 is an R^d-valued random variable. No moment bound on U_0^eta is assumed.

For t in [0,T], put k=floor(t/eta) and r=t/eta-k. Define the piecewise-linear interpolation by

    hat theta^eta(t)=(1-r)theta_k^eta+r theta_{k+1}^eta,

with the value at a grid point understood to be theta_k^eta.

## Task

Prove that, as eta decreases to zero,

    sup_{0<=t<=T} ||hat theta^eta(t)-bar theta(t)|| -> 0

in probability. Prove also that

    U^eta(t)=sqrt(b/eta)(hat theta^eta(t)-bar theta(t))

converges weakly in D([0,T];R^d), equipped with the Skorokhod J1 topology, to the solution of

    dU_t=-D h(bar theta(t))U_t dt+C*(bar theta(t))dW_t,
    U(0)=U_0,

where C*(theta) is the symmetric positive-semidefinite square root of G*(theta), and W is a standard d-dimensional Brownian motion independent of U_0. Establish uniqueness of this solution law. Since the interpolated processes and the limit are continuous, also establish convergence in C([0,T];R^d) with its uniform topology.

As part of the proof, derive for each fixed theta in U the mini-batch covariance identity

    Cov(Pi_theta g_B(theta))=G*(theta)/b.
