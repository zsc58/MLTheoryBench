# Problem

## Setup

For each n, observe vectors y_i in R^p generated by
\[
y_i=a_{z_i}+\xi_i,\qquad i=1,\ldots,n,
\]
where the labels z_i belong to {1,...,K} and are fixed, every component is nonempty, and the noises xi_i are independent N(0,I_p). Let A=[a_1,...,a_K] in R^{p x K} have full column rank, and let Z in R^{n x K} be the one-hot membership matrix. With Y=[y_1,...,y_n] and N=[xi_1,...,xi_n], we have Y=AZ^T+N.

For K>=2 define
\[
\Delta=\min_{k\ne\ell}\|a_k-a_\ell\|.
\]
For all K define n_k=|\{i:z_i=k\}|,
\[
\beta=\frac{\min_k n_k}{n/K}\in(0,1],\qquad
\kappa=\frac{\sigma_1(A)}{\sigma_K(A)}.
\]
Assume K<=min(p,n) and consider n->infinity, allowing p=p_n, K, and beta to vary with n. Let s_n>0 satisfy s_n->infinity and s_n=o(sqrt(p)+sqrt(n)). Define
\[
H=I_n-\frac{1_n1_n^T}{n},\qquad Y_c=YH,
\]
and let widehat sigma_1>=widehat sigma_2>=... be the singular values of Y_c. Set
\[
\tau=\sqrt p+\sqrt n+s_n,\qquad
\widehat K=1+\#\{j:\widehat\sigma_j>\tau\}.
\]

## Task

Prove that the following integrated claim holds. If K=1, then Pr(K-hat=1)->1 without any separation assumption. If K>=2 and
\[
\Delta\ge 2\sqrt2\,\frac{\kappa}{\sqrt\beta}\sqrt{\frac K n}\,\tau,
\]
then Pr(K-hat=K)->1.
