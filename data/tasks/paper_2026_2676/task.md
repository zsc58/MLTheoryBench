# Problem

## Setup

Fix an integer dimension \(d\ge 1\) and endpoint vectors \(a,b\in\mathbb N_0^d\).  Write \(e_i\) for the \(i\)-th coordinate vector, \((u)_+=\max\{u,0\}\), and \(s_i=\operatorname{sgn}(b_i-a_i)\).  For \(0\le t\le 1\), let \(B_t^{(1)},\ldots,B_t^{(d)}\) be independent random variables with \(B_t^{(i)}\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(|b_i-a_i|,t)\), and define the count-valued bridge
\[
Y_t^{(i)}=a_i+s_iB_t^{(i)}.
\]
Thus its conditional mass function is the product binomial law \(p_t(z\mid a,b)=\Pr(Y_t=z)\) on \(\mathbb N_0^d\).

Consider a time-inhomogeneous continuous-time Markov chain \(Z_t\) on \(\mathbb N_0^d\), started at \(Z_0=a\), whose only possible jumps at time \(t<1\) are \(z\to z+e_i\) and \(z\to z-e_i\), with coordinate rates \(\lambda_{t,i}(z\mid a,b)\) and \(\mu_{t,i}(z\mid a,b)\), respectively.

## Task

Prove that the bridge law is exactly generated by the local rates
\[
\lambda_{t,i}(z\mid a,b)=\frac{(b_i-z_i)_+}{1-t},\qquad
\mu_{t,i}(z\mid a,b)=\frac{(z_i-b_i)_+}{1-t},
\qquad 0\le t<1,
\]
meaning that \(Z_t\) has mass function \(p_t(\cdot\mid a,b)\) for every \(t<1\) (and consequently converges to \(b\) as \(t\uparrow1\)).
