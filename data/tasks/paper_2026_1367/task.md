# Problem

## Setup

Consider two observed blocks \(x_1\in\mathbb R^{p_1}\) and \(x_2\in\mathbb R^{p_2}\), generated by
\[
x_k=W_{Jk}z+W_{Ik}b_k+\varepsilon_k,\qquad k=1,2.
\]
Let \(r_J,q_1,q_2,q_1^*,q_2^*\) be nonnegative integers, with
\[
z\sim N(0,I_{r_J}),\qquad b_k\sim N(0,I_{q_k}),
\]
and all latent scores and errors mutually independent. The loading matrices satisfy
\[
W_{Jk}\in\mathbb R^{p_k\times r_J},\qquad W_{Ik}\in\mathbb R^{p_k\times q_k},
\]
and the errors satisfy
\[
\varepsilon_k\sim N(0,\sigma_k^2I_{p_k}),\qquad \sigma_k^2>0,
\]
for \(k=1,2\). Assume \(\operatorname{rank}(W_{Jk})=r_J\) and \(\operatorname{rank}([W_{Jk},W_{Ik}])<p_k\) for each block. Write \(A\succeq0\) for positive semidefiniteness.

A second parameterization \((W_{Jk}^*,W_{Ik}^*,\sigma_k^{*2})\) has
\[
W_{Jk}^*\in\mathbb R^{p_k\times r_J},\qquad W_{Ik}^*\in\mathbb R^{p_k\times q_k^*},\qquad b_k^*\sim N(0,I_{q_k^*}),
\]
with \(\varepsilon_k^*\sim N(0,\sigma_k^{*2}I_{p_k})\) and \(\sigma_k^{*2}>0\), and obeys the same model and rank assumptions. Two parameterizations are observationally equivalent when they induce the same joint Gaussian density of \((x_1,x_2)\).

## Task

Prove that the two parameterizations are observationally equivalent if and only if \(\sigma_k^{*2}=\sigma_k^2\) for \(k=1,2\), and there exists an invertible \(r_J\times r_J\) matrix \(T_1\), with \(T_2=(T_1^{-1})^{\mathsf T}\), such that
\[
W_{J1}^*=W_{J1}T_1,\qquad W_{J2}^*=W_{J2}T_2,
\]
and
\[
W_{I1}^*W_{I1}^{*\mathsf T}
=W_{I1}W_{I1}^{\mathsf T}+W_{J1}(I-T_1T_1^{\mathsf T})W_{J1}^{\mathsf T}\succeq0,
\]
\[
W_{I2}^*W_{I2}^{*\mathsf T}
=W_{I2}W_{I2}^{\mathsf T}+W_{J2}(I-T_2T_2^{\mathsf T})W_{J2}^{\mathsf T}\succeq0.
\]
Thus the isotropic error variances (and the joint rank) are identifiable, whereas the split between joint and individual covariance components need not be unique.
