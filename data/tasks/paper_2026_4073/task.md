# Problem

## Setup

Let Omega_X be a nonempty covariate domain, and let k:Omega_X x Omega_X -> R be a positive-semidefinite kernel. Fix an integer n>=1 and a nonempty set Z subset Omega_X x R of admissible labeled records. A dataset is a sequence D=(X,y) in Z^n; repeated records and covariates are allowed. The domain Z, kernel k, signal scale sigma>0, and effective ridge parameter r^2>0 are fixed independently of the private dataset.

A zero-mean Gaussian-process prior has covariance sigma^2 k. For any finite labeled dataset D=(X,y), define

mu_D(x)=k_X(x)^T(K_XX+r^2 I)^(-1)y,

k_D(x,x')=k(x,x')-k_X(x)^T(K_XX+r^2 I)^(-1)k_X(x'),

where k_X(x)=(k(x_i,x))_i and K_XX=(k(x_i,x_j))_{i,j}. For an empty dataset, set mu_empty=0 and k_empty=k. The release mechanism samples one function f_D from the Gaussian process with mean mu_D and covariance sigma^2 k_D.

Two datasets are replace-one neighbors when they differ in one labeled record. For a neighboring pair, let D_- denote the common n-1 record core and let H_- be the RKHS of k_{D_-}.

The covariance formula does not depend on the responses; write k_{X_-} for that formula with covariate sequence X_-. Define

V_n(r)=sup_{X_- in Omega_X^(n-1)} sup_{x in Omega_X} k_{X_-}(x,x),

and

Delta_n(r)=sup_{D,D' in Z^n: D~D'} ||mu_D-mu_D'||_{H_-}.

The unique length-zero covariate sequence is used when n=1. If there are no neighboring pairs, set Delta_n(r)=0. Assume these suprema are finite.

The output space is R^(Omega_X), equipped with the canonical cylinder sigma-algebra generated by all evaluation maps f -> f(x). For alpha>1, the Rényi divergence of probability laws P,Q on a common measurable space is

D_alpha(P||Q)=(alpha-1)^(-1) log integral (dP/dQ)^alpha dQ

when P is absolutely continuous with respect to Q, and is +infinity otherwise. A mechanism is (alpha,epsilon)-RDP when the divergence between its output laws is at most epsilon for every ordered neighboring pair.

For tau>=0 and 1<alpha<1+1/tau, define

psi_alpha(tau)=max{ (1/2)log(1+tau) - [2(alpha-1)]^(-1)log((1+alpha tau)/(1+tau)),
 -(1/2)log(1+tau) - [2(alpha-1)]^(-1)log(1-(alpha-1)tau) }.

When tau=0, interpret the upper bound on alpha as infinity. Likewise, interpret 1+r^2/V_n(r) as infinity when V_n(r)=0.

## Task

Prove the following theorem. For every order

1<alpha<1+r^2/V_n(r),

the posterior sample-path release mechanism is (alpha,epsilon_alpha)-RDP on the canonical cylinder sigma-algebra, where

epsilon_alpha=2 psi_alpha(V_n(r)/r^2)+[alpha/2] [(V_n(r)+r^2)/(r^2-(alpha-1)V_n(r))] (Delta_n(r)/sigma)^2.

Equivalently, for every ordered replace-one neighboring pair D,D', every integer m>=1, and every test sequence (x_1,...,x_m) in Omega_X^m, the Rényi divergence of (f_D(x_1),...,f_D(x_m)) from the corresponding vector under D' is at most epsilon_alpha.
