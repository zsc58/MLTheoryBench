# Problem

## Setup

Fix integers \(p\ge2\) and \(n\ge1\). For each cluster \(i=1,\ldots,m\), observe a matrix \(Y^{(i)}\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}\) generated by
\[
Y^{(i)}=Y^{(i)}(A_0+Z^{(i)})+E^{(i)}.
\]
The entries of \(E^{(i)}\) are independent \(N(0,1)\). Independently of \(E^{(i)}\), the entries of \(Z^{(i)}\) are mutually independent with
\[
Z^{(i)}_{jk}\sim N(0,R_{0,jk}),
\]
where \(R_0\in\mathbb R_+^{p\times p}\). The pairs \((E^{(i)},Z^{(i)})\) are independent and identically distributed across clusters. Each matrix \(Z^{(i)}\) is shared by all \(n\) observations in its cluster. The diagonals of \(A_0\) and \(R_0\) are zero.

For a matrix \(C\), let \(\mathcal G(C)\) contain the directed edge \(j\to k\) exactly when \(C_{jk}\ne0\). For a nonnegative matrix this is equivalent to \(C_{jk}>0\). Assume the union \(\mathcal G(A_0)\cup\mathcal G(R_0)\) is a DAG. The structural equations are interpreted recursively along a topological order.

Use the entrywise matrix norms
\[
\|C\|_1=\sum_{j,k}|C_{jk}|,\qquad
\|C\|_\infty=\max_{j,k}|C_{jk}|,\qquad
\|C\|_F^2=\sum_{j,k}C_{jk}^2,
\]
and set \(\|(A,R)\|_F^2=\|A\|_F^2+\|R\|_F^2\).

For candidate matrices \(A\in\mathbb R^{p\times p}\) and \(R\in\mathbb R_+^{p\times p}\) with zero diagonals, write \(a_k,r_k\) for their \(k\)-th columns and \(Y_k^{(i)}\) for the \(k\)-th column of \(Y^{(i)}\). Define
\[
V_k^{(i)}(r_k)=I_n+Y^{(i)}\operatorname{diag}(r_k)Y^{(i)\mathsf T}
\]
and the loss
\[
Q_m(A,R)=\frac1{2m}\sum_{i=1}^m\sum_{k=1}^p
\left\{\log\det V_k^{(i)}(r_k)
+(Y_k^{(i)}-Y^{(i)}a_k)^{\mathsf T}V_k^{(i)}(r_k)^{-1}
(Y_k^{(i)}-Y^{(i)}a_k)\right\}.
\]
On candidates whose union graph is a DAG, this is the normalized negative log-likelihood up to an additive constant. The displayed formula defines \(Q_m\) on all zero-diagonal candidates with \(R\ge0\). Let \(Q(A,R)=\mathbb E[Q_m(A,R)]\), where the expectation is under the true model.

Choose finite constants \(M_A>\|A_0\|_\infty\) and \(M_R>\|R_0\|_\infty\), and let
\[
\mathcal A=\{(A,R):\|A\|_\infty\le M_A,\ 0\le R_{jk}\le M_R,
\ \operatorname{diag}(A)=\operatorname{diag}(R)=0,
\ \mathcal G(A)\cup\mathcal G(R)\text{ is a DAG}\}.
\]
For positive deterministic sequences \(\lambda_{1m},\lambda_{2m}\), let \((\widehat A,\widehat R)\) be any measurable choice of a global minimizer over \(\mathcal A\) of
\[
F_m(A,R)=Q_m(A,R)+\lambda_{1m}\|A\|_1+\lambda_{2m}\|R\|_1.
\]

Let \(\mathcal I=\{(j,k):j\ne k\}\),
\[
T_A=\{(j,k)\in\mathcal I:A_{0,jk}\ne0\},\qquad
T_R=\{(j,k)\in\mathcal I:R_{0,jk}>0\},
\]
and assume both sets are nonempty. Vectorize the off-diagonal coordinates as \(\theta=(A,R)\), with fixed-effect and random-effect coordinates distinguished even when their matrix indices agree. Let \(T\) denote the active coordinates, ordered as \((A_{T_A},R_{T_R})\), and put
\[
H=\nabla^2Q(A_0,R_0).
\]
Derivatives refer to the displayed ambient loss formula in these off-diagonal coordinates; derivatives at zero random-effect coordinates are right derivatives. Assume \(H\) is positive definite. Let \(H_{TT}\) be its active principal submatrix, let \(s_A=(\operatorname{sign}A_{0,jk})_{(j,k)\in T_A}\), and let \(h^{(A)}_{jk,T}\) and \(h^{(R)}_{jk,T}\) be the rows of \(H\) joining, respectively, an inactive fixed-effect or random-effect coordinate to \(T\).

Assume
\[
\lambda_{1m},\lambda_{2m}\to0,\qquad
\sqrt m\,\lambda_{1m},\sqrt m\,\lambda_{2m}\to\infty,
\qquad \frac{\lambda_{2m}}{\lambda_{1m}}\to\kappa\in(0,\infty).
\]
Finally, suppose that for some \(\eta\in(0,1)\),
\[
\left|h^{(A)}_{jk,T}H_{TT}^{-1}
\binom{s_A}{\kappa\mathbf1}\right|\le1-\eta
\quad\text{for every }(j,k)\in\mathcal I\setminus T_A,
\]
and
\[
h^{(R)}_{jk,T}H_{TT}^{-1}
\binom{s_A}{\kappa\mathbf1}\le\kappa(1-\eta)
\quad\text{for every }(j,k)\in\mathcal I\setminus T_R,
\]
where \(\mathbf1\) has \(|T_R|\) entries.

## Task

Prove the following integrated theorem.

1. Within the stated Gaussian mixed-effects structural-equation model, the pair \((A_0,R_0)\) is identifiable from the distribution of the observed clustered data. Consequently \(\mathcal G(A_0)\), \(\mathcal G(R_0)\), and their union are identifiable.

2. As \(m\to\infty\), every global minimizer satisfies
\[
\|(\widehat A,\widehat R)-(A_0,R_0)\|_F\xrightarrow{p}0,
\]
and
\[
\Pr\!\left(\mathcal G(\widehat A)=\mathcal G(A_0)
\text{ and }\mathcal G(\widehat R)=\mathcal G(R_0)\right)\longrightarrow1.
\]
