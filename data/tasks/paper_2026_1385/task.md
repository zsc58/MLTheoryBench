# Problem

## Setup

Let (X,Y) be a random pair on a measurable space, and let the algorithm observe i.i.d. copies of this pair. Let beta in R^p and theta in R^q. Suppose f(X,Y,beta) and Psi(X,theta) take values in R^m and g:R^m -> R. Define

F(X,beta)=E[f(X,Y,beta)|X],
G(beta)=E[g(F(X,beta))],
Q(beta,theta)=(1/2)E[||F(X,beta)-Psi(X,theta)||^2].

For every beta, assume some theta satisfies Psi(X,theta)=F(X,beta) almost surely. Assume G attains its minimum G^min.

Assume the following conditions, with all stated bounds holding over every parameter value:

(A1) The function g is convex and twice continuously differentiable. There are finite constants L_g, L_nabla g, and L_nabla^2 g such that ||nabla g(w)||<=L_g, ||nabla^2 g(w)||<=L_nabla g, and ||nabla^2 g(w)-nabla^2 g(w')||<=L_nabla^2 g ||w-w'|| for all w,w' in R^m.

(A2) The function f(x,y,.) is differentiable. There are nonnegative measurable functions L_f(x,y), L_nabla f(x,y), and finite positive constants bar L_f, bar L_nabla f, C_f such that, for all beta,beta', ||nabla_beta f(x,y,beta)||<=L_f(x,y), ||nabla_beta f(x,y,beta)-nabla_beta f(x,y,beta')||<=L_nabla f(x,y)||beta-beta'||; and, for each s in {2,4}, E[L_f(X,Y)^s]<=bar L_f^s, E[L_nabla f(X,Y)^s]<=bar L_nabla f^s, and E[||f(X,Y,beta)||^s]<=C_f^s.

(A3) The function Psi(x,.) is differentiable. There are nonnegative measurable functions L_Psi(x), L_nabla Psi(x), and finite positive constants bar L_Psi, bar L_nabla Psi, C_Psi such that, for all theta,theta', ||nabla_theta Psi(x,theta)||<=L_Psi(x), ||nabla_theta Psi(x,theta)-nabla_theta Psi(x,theta')||<=L_nabla Psi(x)||theta-theta'||; and, for each s in {2,4}, E[L_Psi(X)^s]<=bar L_Psi^s, E[L_nabla Psi(X)^s]<=bar L_nabla Psi^s, and E[||Psi(X,theta)||^s]<=C_Psi^s. Jacobians in the displayed update below are transposed Jacobians, so their products with vectors have dimensions p and q respectively. Under these conditions F is differentiable in beta with nabla_beta F(X,beta)=E[nabla_beta f(X,Y,beta)|X], and G and Q have the derivatives used below.

(A4) There is M>0 such that Q(beta,theta)<=M||nabla_theta Q(beta,theta)||^2 for every beta,theta.

Start from a fixed finite (beta^0,theta^0). At iteration k, draw a fresh (X^k,Y^k), independent of the history F_k generated by the initial point and all observations and iterates preceding this draw. Set

f^k=f(X^k,Y^k,beta^k), f_beta^k=nabla_beta f(X^k,Y^k,beta^k), Psi^k=Psi(X^k,theta^k), Psi_theta^k=nabla_theta Psi(X^k,theta^k),

tilde d_beta^k=-f_beta^k nabla g(Psi^k), tilde d_theta^k=gamma Psi_theta^k(f^k-Psi^k),

beta^{k+1}=beta^k+tau_k tilde d_beta^k, theta^{k+1}=theta^k+tau_k tilde d_theta^k,

where gamma>0. For positive c_1,c_2 define V(beta,theta)=c_1 Q(beta,theta)+c_2||nabla_beta G(beta)||^2. For lambda>=0 define

Delta^lambda(beta,theta)=E[g(F)-g(Psi)-nabla g(Psi)^T(F-Psi)+(lambda/2)||F-Psi||^2], and W^lambda=G+Delta^lambda,

where F=F(X,beta) and Psi=Psi(X,theta) inside this expectation.

## Task

Prove that there are lambda>max{L_nabla g,2M bar L_Psi^2 L_nabla g}, a threshold gamma_min>0, and positive constants c_1,c_2 depending only on the stated problem constants such that the following holds for every gamma>gamma_min. For every integer N>=1 and alpha>0, run the algorithm with tau_k=alpha/sqrt(N) for k=0,...,N-1. Let S be independent of the observations and iterates and uniformly distributed on {0,...,N-1}. Then

E[V(beta^S,theta^S)] <= { (L_W/2)(C_d^2+sigma^2)alpha^2+W^lambda(beta^0,theta^0)-G^min }/(alpha sqrt(N)),

where L_W,C_d,sigma are finite constants that may depend on the stated regularity and moment constants and on the fixed choices of lambda and gamma, but not on k,N,alpha. Hence, for fixed alpha and gamma, E[V(beta^S,theta^S)]=O(N^(-1/2)).
