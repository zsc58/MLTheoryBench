# Problem

## Setup

Fix an input x in R^{d_0}, a network layer with activation map f:R^{d_0}->R^d, and an output class with output map h_k:R^d->R. The network maps are deterministic, and h_k is differentiable at f(x). For a random concept activation vector v in R^d, the scalar sensitivity is
\[
S=\langle\nabla h_k(f(x)),v\rangle.
\]
All randomness below concerns the sensitivity at this fixed input, not an additional average over inputs.

Let mu be real and sigma>0. Assume the sensitivity generated by a full budget of N samples is modeled as
\[
Z_N\sim\mathcal N(\mu,\sigma^2/N).
\]
Split the budget as N=sn, where s>=2 and n>=1 are integers, and assume the s batch sensitivities Z_{n,1},...,Z_{n,s} are independent N(mu,sigma^2/n) variables. Let Phi be the standard normal cdf, let g(t)=(1+e^{-t})^{-1}, and set tau_1=pi/8 and tau_2=0.358.

Define the standard hard score, Multi-TCAV score, and smooth score by
\[
I_N=\mathbf1\{Z_N>0\},\qquad
M_s=\frac1s\sum_{b=1}^s\mathbf1\{Z_{n,b}>0\},\qquad
A_\alpha=g(\alpha Z_N),\quad \alpha>0.
\]

In the approximate part of the task, the displayed smooth-moment formulas and sigmoid–probit relation are adopted approximation prescriptions. The symbol approximately does not assert an exact identity, a quantified error bound, or asymptotic equivalence. The requested limit concerns the displayed approximate ratio expression, not a limit assertion for the exact variance ratio.

## Task

Establish the following exact distributional statements and approximation-based comparison.

The standard hard score is Bernoulli with parameter
\[
\Phi(\sqrt N\,\mu/\sigma),
\]
while Multi-TCAV is normalized-binomial with mean
\[
\Phi(\sqrt n\,\mu/\sigma)
\]
and variance
\[
\frac{\Phi(\sqrt n\,\mu/\sigma)[1-\Phi(\sqrt n\,\mu/\sigma)]}{s}.
\]

Under the adopted logit-normal moment approximation, the smooth score has approximate mean and variance
\[
\mathbb E A_\alpha\approx m_\alpha
:=g\!\left(\frac{\alpha\mu}{\sqrt{1+\tau_1\alpha^2\sigma^2/N}}\right),
\]
\[
\operatorname{Var}(A_\alpha)\approx
m_\alpha(1-m_\alpha)
\left[1-\left(1+\frac{\tau_2\alpha^2\sigma^2}{N}\right)^{-1/2}\right].
\]
Use these prescriptions together with the sigmoid–probit approximation
\[
g(t)\approx\Phi(\sqrt{\tau_1}\,t).
\]

Show that the positive parameter
\[
\alpha_\star=\frac1\sigma\sqrt{\frac{n}{\tau_1(1-1/s)}}
\]
approximately matches the Multi-TCAV mean and that its resulting approximate variance ratio is
\[
\frac{\operatorname{Var}(A_{\alpha_\star})}{\operatorname{Var}(M_s)}
\approx
s\left[1-\left(1+\frac{\tau_2/\tau_1}{s-1}\right)^{-1/2}\right].
\]
Prove that the expression on the right tends to
\[
\frac{\tau_2}{2\tau_1}\approx0.4558
\qquad\text{as the number of batches }s\to\infty.
\]
Thus, within this approximation, the mean-matched smooth score retains about 45.6% of the Multi-TCAV variance, consistent with a roughly 45–55% variance level for large numbers of batches.
