# Problem

## Setup

Let \(m,d\) be positive integers, let \(\mathcal P\subseteq\mathbb R^m\) be a bounded position space, and let \(\mathcal A\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) be an action set containing \(0\). Equip these spaces with their inherited Borel sigma-algebras. Let \(\mathcal W\) be a context set, and for each \(W\in\mathcal W\) let \(g_W\in\mathbb R^m\) be its goal position. All norms are Euclidean norms in the appropriate space. Let
\[
F:\mathcal P\times\mathcal A\times\mathcal W\to\mathcal P
\]
be a transition map satisfying \(F(x,0,W)=x\), with \(F(\cdot,\cdot,W)\) measurable for each \(W\). Fix a discount factor \(\delta\in(0,1)\), a tolerance \(\theta\ge0\), and a finite nonnegative integer step limit \(H\).

Let \(\mathcal O\) be a measurable observation space. For each \(W\), let \(K_W\) be a probability kernel from \(\mathcal P\) to \(\mathcal O\), describing the observation distribution. Let \(\mu:\mathcal O\to\mathcal A\) be a measurable deterministic, stateless policy. During an episode the context stays fixed. Given the current position \(X_t\) and the past, an observation \(o_t\) has conditional law \(K_W(X_t,\cdot)\); the action and next position are
\[
B_t=\mu(o_t),\qquad X_{t+1}=F(X_t,B_t,W).
\]
Thus the policy acts on its current observation, not directly on the latent position or context.

For an episode started at \(X_0=x\) with context \(W\), termination occurs at
\[
T=\min\bigl\{H,\inf\{t\ge0:\|X_t-g_W\|\le\theta\}\bigr\},
\]
where the infimum of the empty set is \(+\infty\). The reward for an executed action at time \(t<T\) is \(-\|X_{t+1}-g_W\|\). Define the discounted state-value function by
\[
J(x,W)=\mathbb E^\mu_{x,W}\!\left[-\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\delta^t\|X_{t+1}-g_W\|\right],
\]
where the expectation is over the observation draws for an episode started at \(x\) with the fixed context \(W\), and an empty sum is zero.

Call \(\mu\) *distance-improving* if, for every context, starting position, and episode trajectory it generates, each later visited position is strictly closer to the goal than each earlier visited position: for all visited indices \(0\le p<q\le T\),
\[
\|X_q-g_W\|<\|X_p-g_W\|.
\]

For \(x^+=F(x,u,W)\), an action \(u\in\mathcal A\) is a \(\mu\)-shortcut at \((x,W)\) when
\[
\delta J(x^+,W)-J(x,W)\ge\|x^+-g_W\|.
\]

Assume the following placement-error property with one real constant \(c_F\ge0\) common to all contexts and action chains. For every \(W\in\mathcal W\), every positive integer \(r\), and every executable action chain \(b_0,\ldots,b_{r-1}\in\mathcal A\) from \(x_0\in\mathcal P\), with \(x_{k+1}=F(x_k,b_k,W)\), whenever \(\bar b=\sum_{k=0}^{r-1}b_k\in\mathcal A\),
\[
\bigl\|F(x_0,\bar b,W)-x_r\bigr\|
\le c_F\sum_{k=0}^{r-1}\|b_k\|.
\]
Assume also that \(J\) is Lipschitz in position with one real constant \(L\ge0\) common to all contexts:
\[
|J(x,W)-J(x',W)|\le L\|x-x'\|
\qquad\text{for all }W\in\mathcal W\text{ and }x,x'\in\mathcal P.
\]

## Task

Prove the following claim. Suppose \(\mu\) is distance-improving. Fix a context \(W\), write \(g=g_W\), and let \(x_i,\ldots,x_j\) be a segment of an episode generated by \(\mu\) at this context, with visited indices \(0\le i<j\le T\). Let \(b_i,\ldots,b_{j-1}\) be the actions taken by \(\mu\) on this segment, so that
\[
x_{k+1}=F(x_k,b_k,W)\qquad(i\le k<j).
\]
Define
\[
u=\sum_{k=i}^{j-1}b_k,
\]
and suppose \(u\in\mathcal A\). If
\[
\delta J(x_j,W)-J(x_i,W)-\|x_j-g\|
\ge(\delta L+1)c_F\sum_{k=i}^{j-1}\|b_k\|,
\]
then \(u\) is a \(\mu\)-shortcut at \((x_i,W)\).
