# Problem

## Setup

Let \(d\ge1\). Let \(P,D\in\mathbb R^{d\times d}\) be fixed symmetric positive-definite matrices and let \(Q\in\mathbb R^{d\times d}\) be fixed with \(Q^\top=-Q\). Set
\[
H(x)=\tfrac12x^\top Px,\qquad A=(D+Q)P,
\]
and let \(\sigma\in\mathbb R^{d\times d}\) be fixed with \(\sigma\sigma^\top=2D\). Assume the drift \(g(x)=-Ax\) is one-sided dissipative: there are \(m>0,b\ge0\) with \(\langle x,g(x)\rangle\le-m\|x\|^2+b\) for all \(x\in\mathbb R^d\). Here \(\|\cdot\|\) is the Euclidean norm.

Let \((\mathsf Z,\mathcal Z)\) be a measurable space and let \(\widehat G:\mathbb R^d\times\mathsf Z\to\mathbb R^d\) be a measurable stochastic-gradient oracle. For each \(0<\eta<1\), consider an initial state \(X_0\), Gaussian random vectors \((\xi_k)_{k\ge1}\), and oracle random variables \((\zeta_k)_{k\ge1}\) taking values in \(\mathsf Z\). Their dependence on \(\eta\) is suppressed in the notation. Let \(\mathcal F_k\) be the filtration generated by \(X_0\) and \(\{\xi_j,\zeta_j:1\le j\le k\}\). Assume that \(X_0\) is independent of the entire sequence \((\xi_j,\zeta_j)_{j\ge1}\), and that each \(\xi_{k+1}\) has law \(N(0,I_d)\) and is independent of \(\mathcal F_k\).

Define recursively
\[
\varepsilon_{k+1}=-(D+Q)\bigl[\widehat G(X_k,\zeta_{k+1})-PX_k\bigr],
\]
\[
X_{k+1}=X_k+\eta g(X_k)+\sqrt\eta\,\sigma\xi_{k+1}+\eta\varepsilon_{k+1}.
\]
Assume that the oracle is Markovian: for every bounded Borel function \(f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R\),
\[
\mathbb E[f(\widehat G(X_k,\zeta_{k+1}))\mid\mathcal F_k]
=\mathbb E[f(\widehat G(X_k,\zeta_{k+1}))\mid X_k]
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
For every \(k\ge0\) and \(0<\eta<1\), assume
\[
\mathbb E[\varepsilon_{k+1}\mid\mathcal F_k]=0,
\]
and that \(\varepsilon_{k+1}\) and \(\xi_{k+1}\) are conditionally independent given \(\mathcal F_k\). Suppose there exist \(p>4\) and finite constants \(C_0,C_p\), independent of \(k\) and \(\eta\), such that
\[
\sup_{0<\eta<1}\mathbb E\|X_0\|^p\le C_0,
\qquad
\mathbb E[\|\varepsilon_{k+1}\|^p\mid\mathcal F_k]
\le C_p(1+\|X_k\|^p)
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]

Let \(\mu=N(0,P^{-1})\), \(N_\eta=\lfloor\eta^{-2}\rfloor\), and for a fixed \(u\in\mathbb R^d\) define \(h(x)=u^\top x\), \(\mu(h)=\int h(x)\,\mu(dx)\), and
\[
\widehat m_\eta=\frac1{N_\eta}\sum_{k=0}^{N_\eta-1}h(X_k).
\]

## Task

Prove that, as \(\eta\downarrow0\),
\[
\eta^{-1/2}\bigl(\widehat m_\eta-\mu(h)\bigr)\Rightarrow N\!\left(0,V(h)\right),
\]
where \(\mu(h)=0\) and
\[
V(h)=2u^\top A^{-1}D(A^{-1})^\top u.
\]
In the reversible case \(Q=0\), show that this reduces to
\[
V_0(h)=2u^\top(DP)^{-1}D\bigl((DP)^{-1}\bigr)^\top u.
\]
The conclusion must hold for every fixed \(u\) and every stochastic-gradient oracle family satisfying the stated assumptions; in particular, its fixed-variance noise does not change the leading variance. Here \(\Rightarrow\) denotes convergence in distribution, and \(N(0,0)\) denotes the point mass at zero.
