# Problem

## Setup

Fix positive integers d_s, d_a, and H. Consider a finite-horizon episodic MDP with state space S=R^{d_s}, a nonempty Borel action set A contained in the Euclidean ball of radius R_a in R^{d_a}, and a known Borel reward function r satisfying |r(s,a)|<=R_max. The constants R_a, R_max, C, L, and sigma are positive, and alpha is in (0,1].

The true transition mean f*:R^{d_s+d_a}->R^{d_s} is drawn once at the beginning of the interaction. Its coordinates are independent zero-mean Gaussian processes with common symmetric positive-semidefinite kernel c, defined on all of R^{d_s+d_a}, satisfying

    0<c(x,x)<=C,
    |c(x,y)-c(x,z)|<=L||y-z||_2^alpha

for all x,y,z in R^{d_s+d_a}. Use measurable, separable versions of these processes and their posterior draws. Here separability means that on each bounded input domain the process is almost surely determined by its limits along a countable subset, so its suprema agree with suprema over a countable determining set.

Interaction lasts N episodes, with T=NH visited state-action pairs. At episode n, the initial state s_{n,1} has law N(0,sigma^2 I_{d_s}). For h=1,...,H-1, after action a_{n,h} is chosen, the next state is

    s_{n,h+1}=f*(s_{n,h},a_{n,h})+epsilon_{n,h+1}.

The initial-state draws and transition noises are mutually independent N(0,sigma^2 I_{d_s}) vectors, independent of f* and of fresh controller randomization. No next state is generated or observed at h=H.

Let F_{n-1} be the sigma-algebra generated by the observed states and actions in the first n-1 episodes. Before the initial state of episode n is drawn, draw f^(n) from the Bayesian posterior law of f* given F_{n-1}, using fresh randomness conditionally independent of f*. Form the MDP with transition mean f^(n), choose an optimal measurable time-dependent policy pi_n for that model, and execute it for the episode. The policy selection uses only the history, sampled model, and controller randomization. Assume the optimal policies and measurable selections required by this procedure exist. Actions at step h are drawn from pi_n(da|s_{n,h},h) using fresh randomization. At episode end, update the posterior using exactly the observed transitions with h<H. Thus the posterior has (n-1)(H-1) transition responses before episode n, with observation noise variance sigma^2.

For a model M and policy pi, define

    V^M_{pi,h}(s)=E_{M,pi}[sum_{j=h}^H r(s_j,a_j) | s_h=s],
    V^M_{pi,H+1}=0.

Let M* be the true model and pi* an optimal policy for it. Define Bayesian regret by

    R_T=E[sum_{n=1}^N (V^{M*}_{pi*,1}(s_{n,1})-V^{M*}_{pi_n,1}(s_{n,1}))],

where expectation includes the prior draw, posterior draws, initial states, action randomization, and transition noises.

Enumerate all visited inputs x_{n,h}=(s_{n,h},a_{n,h}), including h=H, in episode order as x_1,...,x_T. Let K_T=(c(x_i,x_j))_{i,j=1}^T. For u>0 define

    gamma_T(sigma^2,u)
      =E[(1/2)log det(I_T+sigma^{-2}K_T)
           *1{max_{t<=T}||x_t||_2<=u}],

under this posterior-sampling interaction process. This definition uses all visited inputs, including terminal inputs without next-state responses.

Set

    D=168 alpha^{-1/2} sqrt(max(C,sigma^2)(d_s+d_a)),
    R=D sqrt(log(10(T+R_a) max(1,L/C))),
    R_tilde=sqrt(R^2+R_a^2).

Assume the following is a real-valued inequality and holds:

    T>=D sqrt(2 log(10D max(1,L/C)(R_a+1))).

## Task

Prove, as T=NH increases with the finite-horizon model fixed, that the controller satisfies

    R_T=O(H sqrt((d_s+d_a) gamma_T(sigma^2,R_tilde) T log T)),

with an implied constant independent of T. Include the probability and expectation arguments needed for the unbounded state space and the Holder-continuous kernel.
