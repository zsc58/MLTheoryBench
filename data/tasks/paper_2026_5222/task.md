# Problem

## Setup

Let \(H,n\) be positive integers, let \(R_{\max}\in[0,\infty)\), and fix \(\delta\in(0,1)\). Consider a finite-horizon layered Markov decision process with a finite action set \(\mathcal A\), finite disjoint state layers \(\mathcal S_0,\ldots,\mathcal S_{H+1}\), decision stages \(1,\ldots,H\), and terminal layer \(\mathcal S_{H+1}\). Rewards satisfy \(R_h\in[0,R_{\max}]\) for \(1\le h\le H\). There is a fixed dummy initial state-action pair \((S_0,A_0)\), with \(R_0=0\). Reward and transition laws are fixed and are the same under evaluation and behavior policies.

Fix an evaluation policy \(\pi\), and let
\[
J(\pi)=\mathbb E_\pi\left[\sum_{h=1}^H R_h\right],\qquad B:=H R_{\max}.
\]
For a real-valued function \(f\) on a state layer and \(\mathcal A\), write
\[
f(s,\pi):=\mathbb E_{A\sim\pi(\cdot\mid s)}[f(s,A)].
\]
At the terminal layer all value-function evaluations are defined to be zero.

Let \(q_{H+1}\equiv0\), and let \(q_h\) be the action-value functions of \(\pi\), indexed so that
\[
q_h(s,a)=\mathbb E\left[R_h+q_{h+1}(S_{h+1},\pi)\mid S_h=s,A_h=a\right],\quad 0\le h\le H.
\]
Here the expectation uses the MDP's one-step reward and transition laws at \((s,a)\); at \(h=0\) it is evaluated at the dummy pair. Thus \(q_0(S_0,A_0)=J(\pi)\). For fixed classes
\[
\mathcal F_h\subseteq\{f:\mathcal S_h\times\mathcal A\to\mathbb R\},\qquad 1\le h\le H,
\]
assume realizability:
\[
q_h\in\mathcal F_h\quad\text{for every }1\le h\le H.
\]

Let \(\{(S_0^{(i)},A_0^{(i)},R_0^{(i)},\ldots,S_{H+1}^{(i)})\}_{i=1}^n\) be \(n\) independent and identically distributed trajectories generated by a fixed, arbitrary behavior policy \(\pi_D\) in this MDP, each beginning at the dummy pair. Set \(u_0^{(i)}=1\). For \(1\le h\le H\) and \(u,v\in\mathbb R^n\), define
\[
\ell_h(v;u):=\sup_{f\in\mathcal F_h}\left|\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n v^{(i)}f(S_h^{(i)},A_h^{(i)})-\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n u^{(i)}f(S_h^{(i)},\pi)\right|.
\]
The supremum is taken in \([0,+\infty]\).

For each \(1\le h\le H\), choose \(u_h\in\mathbb R^n\) by a measurable rule depending only on the joint trajectory prefixes
\[
\left\{(S_0^{(i)},A_0^{(i)},R_0^{(i)},\ldots,S_{h-1}^{(i)},A_{h-1}^{(i)},R_{h-1}^{(i)},S_h^{(i)},A_h^{(i)})\right\}_{i=1}^n.
\]
Define
\[
\widehat J(\pi):=\sum_{h=1}^H\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n u_h^{(i)}R_h^{(i)},
\qquad
\|u_h\|_{[n]}:=\left(\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n (u_h^{(i)})^2\right)^{1/2},\quad 0\le h\le H,
\]
and
\[
\eta_h:=\|u_h\|_{[n]}B\sqrt{\frac{2\log(2(H+1)/\delta)}{n}},\qquad 0\le h\le H.
\]

## Task

For every fixed family of selection rules satisfying these conditions, prove that, with probability at least \(1-\delta\) over the sampled trajectories,
\[
\left|\widehat J(\pi)-J(\pi)\right|
\le
\sum_{h=1}^H\ell_h(u_h;u_{h-1})+\sum_{h=0}^H\eta_h.
\]
