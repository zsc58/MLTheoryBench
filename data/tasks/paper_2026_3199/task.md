# Problem

## Model and notation

Let \(T>0\), \(\beta\ge0\), and let \(\Theta\subset\mathbb R^d\) be open. States lie in \(\mathbb R^n\) and actions in \(\mathbb R^m\). Write \(\mathcal P_2\) for probability laws with finite second moment, equipped with the 2-Wasserstein distance \(\mathcal W_2\), and set \(M_2(\mu)^2=\int|x|^2\mu(dx)\). For matrices, \(M:N=\operatorname{tr}(M^\top N)\).

Work on a filtered probability space supporting a \(k\)-dimensional Brownian motion \(W\) and a sufficiently rich initial sigma-field independent of \(W\). The filtration is generated by that sigma-field and \(W\), with the usual augmentation. Fix an initial variable \(\xi_0\in L^2(\mathcal F_0;\mathbb R^n)\), whose law \(\mu_0\) does not depend on the policy parameter. All laws below are unconditional laws.

Let \(b,\sigma,r\) have domain
\[
[0,T]\times\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^m\times\mathcal P_2(\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^m)
\]
and values in \(\mathbb R^n,\mathbb R^{n\times k},\mathbb R\), respectively. Let \(g:\mathbb R^n\times\mathcal P_2(\mathbb R^n)\to\mathbb R\). These functions are continuous. A deterministic policy \(\varphi_\eta(t,x,\mu)\), \(\eta\in\Theta\), is continuous in its arguments, Lipschitz in \((x,\mu)\) uniformly in time for each fixed parameter, and \(C^1\) in \(\eta\). Define
\[
\Gamma^\eta(t,\mu)=(\operatorname{id},\varphi_\eta(t,\cdot,\mu))_\#\mu,
\qquad
\ell_\eta(t,x,\mu)=\ell(t,x,\varphi_\eta(t,x,\mu),\Gamma^\eta(t,\mu)),\quad \ell\in\{b,\sigma,r\}.
\]
Here \(f_\#\mu\) denotes the push-forward law under \(f\).

Assume the induced coefficients are jointly continuous. For each compact \(K\subset\Theta\), assume there is \(C_K<\infty\) such that, for all \(t\in[0,T]\), \(\eta\in K\), states \(x,y\), and laws \(\mu,\nu\),
\[
|b_\eta(t,x,\mu)-b_\eta(t,y,\nu)|+|\sigma_\eta(t,x,\mu)-\sigma_\eta(t,y,\nu)|
\le C_K(|x-y|+\mathcal W_2(\mu,\nu)),
\]
\[
|b_\eta(t,0,\delta_0)|+|\sigma_\eta(t,0,\delta_0)|\le C_K,
\qquad
|r_\eta(t,x,\mu)|+|g(x,\mu)|\le C_K(1+|x|^2+M_2(\mu)^2).
\]
For \(\xi\in L^2(\mathcal F_t;\mathbb R^n)\) with law \(\mu\), let \(X^{t,\xi,\eta}\) be the unique square-integrable strong solution of
\[
dX_s=b_\eta(s,X_s,\mathcal L(X_s))ds+\sigma_\eta(s,X_s,\mathcal L(X_s))dW_s,\qquad X_t=\xi.
\]
Write \(\mu_s^{t,\mu,\eta}=\mathcal L(X_s^{t,\xi,\eta})\); this law depends on \(\xi\) only through \(\mu\). Define
\[
\bar r(t,\mu,\eta)=\int r_\eta(t,x,\mu)\mu(dx),\qquad \bar g(\mu)=\int g(x,\mu)\mu(dx),
\]
\[
V^\eta(t,\mu)=\int_t^T e^{-\beta(s-t)}\bar r(s,\mu_s^{t,\mu,\eta},\eta)ds+e^{-\beta(T-t)}\bar g(\mu_T^{t,\mu,\eta}),
\qquad J(\varphi_\eta)=V^\eta(0,\mu_0).
\]

## Differentiability assumptions

The Lions derivative \(\partial_\mu w(t,\mu)(x)\) is defined through the Fréchet derivative of the lift \(\xi\mapsto w(t,\mathcal L(\xi))\) on \(L^2\): its derivative in direction \(H\) is \(\mathbb E[\partial_\mu w(t,\mu)(\xi)\cdot H]\), where \(\mathcal L(\xi)=\mu\).

In this problem, \(C^{1,2}\) denotes the following class of functions on \([0,T]\times\mathcal P_2(\mathbb R^n)\). The function and its time derivative are jointly continuous; it is Lions differentiable in the law; its Lions derivative has a continuously differentiable spatial version; and both \(\partial_\mu w(t,\mu)(x)\) and \(\partial_x\partial_\mu w(t,\mu)(x)\) are jointly continuous at points with \(x\in\operatorname{supp}(\mu)\). For every compact \(\mathcal K\subset\mathcal P_2(\mathbb R^n)\),
\[
\sup_{(t,\mu)\in[0,T]\times\mathcal K}\left\{\int|\partial_\mu w(t,\mu)(x)|^2\mu(dx)+\|\partial_x\partial_\mu w(t,\mu)\|_{L^\infty(\mu)}\right\}<\infty.
\]
Time derivatives at the endpoints are one-sided.

For \(w\in C^{1,2}\), define
\[
\mathcal L^\eta[w](t,\mu)=\partial_t w(t,\mu)-\beta w(t,\mu)+\int\left[b_\eta(t,x,\mu)\cdot\partial_\mu w(t,\mu)(x)+\tfrac12\sigma_\eta\sigma_\eta^\top(t,x,\mu):\partial_x\partial_\mu w(t,\mu)(x)\right]\mu(dx),
\]
\[
\mathcal A^\eta[w](t,\mu)=\mathcal L^\eta[w](t,\mu)+\bar r(t,\mu,\eta).
\]
Assume \(V^\eta\in C^{1,2}\) for every \(\eta\in\Theta\). Assume also that, for every fixed \(w\in C^{1,2}\), \(\eta\mapsto\mathcal A^\eta[w](t,\mu)\) is differentiable and its parameter derivative is jointly continuous in \((t,\mu,\eta)\).

## Local candidates

Fix \(\theta\in\Theta\). Let
\[
\widehat V_D:[0,T]\times\mathbb R^n\times\mathcal P_2(\mathbb R^n)\to\mathbb R,
\quad
\widehat q_D:[0,T]\times\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^m\times\mathcal P_2(\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R^m)\to\mathbb R
\]
be continuous. They are fixed candidates associated with this base parameter; varying \(\eta\) below does not vary the candidates themselves. Assume the following integrals are absolutely finite:
\[
\widehat V(t,\mu)=\int\widehat V_D(t,x,\mu)\mu(dx),
\qquad
\widehat q(t,\mu,\eta)=\int\widehat q_D(t,x,\varphi_\eta(t,x,\mu),\Gamma^\eta(t,\mu))\mu(dx).
\]
Assume \(\widehat V\in C^{1,2}\), and assume \(\widehat q\) and \(\partial_\eta\widehat q\) exist and are jointly continuous.

For fixed \((t,x,a)\), \(\partial_\nu^a\widehat q_D(t,x,a,\nu)(y,c)\in\mathbb R^m\) denotes the action-coordinate derivative of the joint-law lift. More precisely, the derivative at \(A\) in direction \(H\) of
\(A\mapsto\widehat q_D(t,x,a,\mathcal L(Z,A))\), with \(Z\) held fixed and \(\mathcal L(Z,A)=\nu\), is
\[
\mathbb E[\partial_\nu^a\widehat q_D(t,x,a,\nu)(Z,A)\cdot H].
\]
Assume the derivatives in this definition and \(\partial_a\widehat q_D\) exist at all arguments used below. For independent \(\xi,\widetilde\xi\sim\mu\), put \(a_\eta=\varphi_\eta(t,\xi,\mu)\), \(\widetilde a_\eta=\varphi_\eta(t,\widetilde\xi,\mu)\), and \(\Gamma=\Gamma^\eta(t,\mu)\). Assume the explicit chain rule
\[
\partial_\eta\widehat q(t,\mu,\eta)=\mathbb E\!\left[\partial_\eta\varphi_\eta(t,\xi,\mu)^\top\left(\partial_a\widehat q_D(t,\xi,a_\eta,\Gamma)+\widetilde{\mathbb E}[\partial_\nu^a\widehat q_D(t,\widetilde\xi,\widetilde a_\eta,\Gamma)(\xi,a_\eta)]\right)\right].
\]
The derivative holds \((t,\mu)\) fixed. The tilde expectation integrates only the independent copy. Assume these expectations are absolutely defined, including the expectation of the norm of each Jacobian-multiplied direct term and the double expectation of the norm of the Jacobian-multiplied joint-law term. Along the base trajectory \(X_s^\theta=X_s^{0,\xi_0,\theta}\), assume these two absolute quantities are integrable over \(s\in[0,T]\).

Assume, for all \(t,x,\mu\),
\[
\widehat V_D(T,x,\mu)=g(x,\mu),\qquad \widehat q(t,\mu,\theta)=0.
\]
For every \((t,\mu)\in[0,T]\times\mathcal P_2(\mathbb R^n)\), assume there is an open neighborhood \(O_{t,\mu}(\theta)\subset\Theta\) of \(\theta\) such that, for every \(\eta\in O_{t,\mu}(\theta)\) and every initial \(\xi\in L^2(\mathcal F_t;\mathbb R^n)\) of law \(\mu\), the following is a true \((\mathcal F_s)\)-martingale on \([t,T]\):
\[
M_s=e^{-\beta s}\widehat V_D(s,X_s^{t,\xi,\eta},\mu_s^{t,\mu,\eta})
+\int_t^s e^{-\beta u}(r-\widehat q_D)(u,X_u^{t,\xi,\eta},\varphi_\eta(u,X_u^{t,\xi,\eta},\mu_u^{t,\mu,\eta}),\Gamma^\eta(u,\mu_u^{t,\mu,\eta}))du.
\]
For these starts and parameters, assume explicitly
\[
\mathbb E\int_t^T\left|\widehat q_D(u,X_u^{t,\xi,\eta},\varphi_\eta(u,X_u^{t,\xi,\eta},\mu_u^{t,\mu,\eta}),\Gamma^\eta(u,\mu_u^{t,\mu,\eta}))\right|du<\infty.
\]
The neighborhoods need not be uniform in \((t,\mu)\).

## Task

Prove
\[
\widehat V(t,\mu)=V^\theta(t,\mu),\qquad (t,\mu)\in[0,T]\times\mathcal P_2(\mathbb R^n),
\]
and
\[
\widehat q(t,\mu,\eta)=\mathcal A^\eta[V^\theta](t,\mu),\qquad t<T,\quad\eta\in O_{t,\mu}(\theta).
\]
At the terminal time, prove the base-parameter equality \(\widehat q(T,\mu,\theta)=\mathcal A^\theta[V^\theta](T,\mu)=0\).

Finally, with \(\mu_s^\theta=\mathcal L(X_s^\theta)\), \(a_s=\varphi_\theta(s,X_s^\theta,\mu_s^\theta)\), \(\Gamma_s^\theta=\Gamma^\theta(s,\mu_s^\theta)\), and \((\widetilde X_s^\theta,\widetilde a_s)\) an independent copy of \((X_s^\theta,a_s)\), prove differentiability of \(\eta\mapsto J(\varphi_\eta)\) at \(\theta\) and the formula
\[
\nabla_\theta J(\varphi_\theta)=\mathbb E\!\left[\int_0^T e^{-\beta s}\partial_\theta\varphi_\theta(s,X_s^\theta,\mu_s^\theta)^\top\left(\partial_a\widehat q_D(s,X_s^\theta,a_s,\Gamma_s^\theta)+\widetilde{\mathbb E}[\partial_\nu^a\widehat q_D(s,\widetilde X_s^\theta,\widetilde a_s,\Gamma_s^\theta)(X_s^\theta,a_s)]\right)ds\right].
\]
