# Problem

## Setup

Let V be a finite vocabulary of size m, and choose \gamma\in(0,1) so that g=\gamma m is an integer. At each position i, sample a green list G_i\subset V of size g independently and uniformly. Under the unwatermarked null, the token sequence is independent of these lists. For a base next-token probability vector \sigma^{(i)}, watermarking adds \delta>0 to the logits of tokens in G_i and leaves all other logits unchanged. Define I_i=\mathbf 1\{x_i\in G_i\} and S_n=\sum_{i=1}^n I_i. The one-sided detector of nominal level \alpha\in(0,1) rejects when
\[
\frac{S_n-n\gamma}{\sqrt{n\gamma(1-\gamma)}}>z_{1-\alpha},
\]
where z_{1-\alpha} is the (1-\alpha)-quantile of the standard normal law and \Phi is its cdf.

For parameter calibration, fix any realized green list G of size g and let \sigma\sim\operatorname{Dirichlet}(1,\ldots,1) on the m-simplex. For sequence-level alternative analysis, assume \{I_i\} is stationary with mean \gamma', the mutual information between the sigma-fields generated by indicators at times at most s and at least t is at most C\rho^{t-s} for constants C>0 and \rho\in(0,1) whenever s<t, and the long-run variance \sigma^2=\lim_{n\to\infty}n^{-1}\operatorname{Var}(S_n) exists and is positive. Assume 0<\gamma'<1.

## Task

Prove the following integrated theorem. The marginal green-token probability under watermarking is
\[
\gamma'=e^\delta\gamma\,{}_2F_1\!\left(1,g+1;m+1;-(e^\delta-1)\right),
\]
where \({}_2F_1\) is the Gauss hypergeometric function, and, as m\to\infty with fixed \gamma,\delta,
\[
\gamma'=\frac{e^\delta\gamma}{1+\gamma(e^\delta-1)}+O(m^{-1/2}).
\]
Moreover, for the detector above, the alternative rejection probability has the normal asymptotic approximation
\[
\Pr_\delta(\mathrm{reject})\approx\Phi\!\left(\frac{\sqrt n(\gamma'-\gamma)-z_{1-\alpha}\sqrt{\gamma(1-\gamma)}}{\sqrt{c\gamma'(1-\gamma')}}\right),
\]
where c>0 is defined by \sigma^2=c\gamma'(1-\gamma'). The approximation is asymptotic in n and is not asserted as an exact finite-sample identity.
