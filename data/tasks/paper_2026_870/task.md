# Problem

## Setup

Fix positive integers N, I_1,...,I_N and a tensor X with entries X_i>=delta_X>0. Fix 0<=beta<2. For x>0 and y>0, define
\[
d_\beta(x\mid y)=\begin{cases}
\bigl(x^\beta+(\beta-1)y^\beta-\beta xy^{\beta-1}\bigr)/[\beta(\beta-1)],&\beta\notin\{0,1\},\\
x/y-\log(x/y)-1,&\beta=0,\\
x\log(x/y)-x+y,&\beta=1.
\end{cases}
\]
Set D_beta(X,Xhat)=sum_i d_beta(X_i|Xhat_i).

Consider either of these models:

- **CP:** Fix a positive integer R. The blocks are matrices A^(n) of size I_n by R, and
\[
\widehat X_{i_1,\ldots,i_N}=\sum_{r=1}^R\prod_{n=1}^N A^{(n)}_{i_nr}.
\]
- **Tucker:** Fix positive integers J_1,...,J_N. The blocks are a core G of size J_1 by ... by J_N and matrices A^(n) of size I_n by J_n, and
\[
\widehat X_i=\sum_{j_1=1}^{J_1}\cdots\sum_{j_N=1}^{J_N}G_{j_1,\ldots,j_N}\prod_{n=1}^N A^{(n)}_{i_nj_n}.
\]

Write Theta for the collection of blocks, and F(Theta)=D_beta(X,Xhat(Theta)). Fix 0<epsilon<=M<infinity. Let C=C_1 times ... times C_B be a nonempty closed convex block-product set, with every parameter entry in [epsilon,M] on C. Here B=N for CP and B=N+1 for Tucker. Let Theta^0 belong to C.

For a reference tilde Theta, a tight joint majorizer S(Theta|tilde Theta) means a function satisfying
\[
S(\Theta\mid\widetilde\Theta)\ge F(\Theta),\qquad S(\widetilde\Theta\mid\widetilde\Theta)=F(\widetilde\Theta),
\]
for all parameter collections with entries at least epsilon. The reference is held fixed while blocks of the first argument are updated.

Construct such a frozen-reference joint surrogate explicitly from the relevant CP or Tucker summands and the beta-divergence formula, and derive its exact epsilon-constrained multiplicative minimizer for every block. The update constraint is entrywise Z>=epsilon. Updates must be expressible using finite indexed tensor contractions and entrywise operations, without tensor unfoldings. At outer iteration k, use reference tilde Theta=Theta^k, initialize the inner state at Theta^k, perform exactly one sweep in a fixed cyclic block order, and call the result Theta^(k+1). Do not insert any additional projection or normalization.

Assume that the outer iterates generated by these epsilon-constrained updates belong to C. This is a containment hypothesis on the generated sequence, not a requirement to establish invariance of arbitrary C. Assume F is continuously differentiable on an open neighborhood of C and has Lipschitz gradient on C. For the constructed S, assume continuous differentiability in its first argument on an open neighborhood of C and a constant L_S<infinity such that
\[
\|\nabla_1S(U\mid V)-\nabla_1S(W\mid V)\|\le L_S\|U-W\|
\quad\text{for all }U,W,V\in C.
\]
Majorization, tightness, exact block optimality, and first-order consistency
\[
\nabla_1S(V\mid V)=\nabla F(V)
\]
are to be proved for the construction, not assumed.

Define Psi=F+iota_C, where iota_C is zero on C and +infinity elsewhere. Use the product Euclidean/Frobenius norm, let partial denote the limiting (Mordukhovich) subdifferential, and let dist(0,E)=inf_{v in E}||v||. Assume Psi has the Kurdyka--Lojasiewicz property: at every bar Theta in dom partial Psi there are eta>0, a neighborhood U of bar Theta, and a continuous concave function phi:[0,eta)->[0,infinity), with phi(0)=0, phi continuously differentiable on (0,eta), and phi'>0, such that
\[
\varphi'(\Psi(\Theta)-\Psi(\bar\Theta))\operatorname{dist}(0,\partial\Psi(\Theta))\ge1
\]
whenever Theta belongs to U and Psi(bar Theta)<Psi(Theta)<Psi(bar Theta)+eta.

## Task

Give the explicit surrogate and block minimizers requested above and prove that the resulting iteration is well defined. Prove, for every k,
\[
F(\Theta^{k+1})\le F(\Theta^k).
\]
Prove that there are constants c_dec,c_err>0, independent of k, such that
\[
\Psi(\Theta^k)-\Psi(\Theta^{k+1})\ge c_{\rm dec}\|\Theta^{k+1}-\Theta^k\|^2,
\qquad
\operatorname{dist}(0,\partial\Psi(\Theta^{k+1}))\le c_{\rm err}\|\Theta^{k+1}-\Theta^k\|.
\]
Conclude that
\[
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\|\Theta^{k+1}-\Theta^k\|<\infty
\]
and that the entire sequence converges to some Theta* in C with 0 in partial Psi(Theta*).
