# Problem

## Setup

Let N >= 2 and 1 <= k <= N-1 be integers. Write [N] = {1,...,N} and

X_k = {A subset of [N] : |A| = k}.

For a symmetric positive semidefinite matrix L, let L_A denote its principal submatrix indexed by A, and assume

Z_k(L) = sum_{A in X_k} det(L_A) > 0.

The k-DPP induced by L is the probability distribution

P_L^k(A) = det(L_A) / Z_k(L),   A in X_k.

Fix a spectral decomposition L = U Lambda U^T, where U is orthogonal and Lambda is diagonal with nonnegative entries. Let c > 0, let D be diagonal with entries in {+1,-1}, and let Q be orthogonal with

Q Lambda Q^T = Lambda.

Define

M = (D U Q)(c Lambda)(D U Q)^T.

For the local statement below, additionally assume that L is positive definite. Let Sym(N) denote the real symmetric N by N matrices. For H in Sym(N), define the directional score

S_H(A;L) = (d/dt) log P^k_{L+tH}(A) at t=0,

where t is restricted to a neighborhood of zero in which L+tH remains positive definite. Define

V_L = {H in Sym(N) : S_H(A;L) = 0 for every A in X_k}.

Here a local non-identifiable direction means an infinitesimal, zero-score direction: an element of V_L.

## Task

Prove that M induces exactly the same k-DPP law as L:

P_M^k(A) = P_L^k(A) for every A in X_k.

Thus the law is invariant under common positive scaling of all eigenvalues, diagonal sign similarity, and orthogonal rotations within equal-eigenvalue eigenspaces.

Prove also that, when L is positive definite,

dim(V_L) >= N(N+1)/2 - binom(N,k) + 1.

Consequently, if binom(N,k) < N(N+1)/2, then V_L has dimension greater than one. Since the tangent direction generated by positive scaling is span{L}, there are local non-identifiable directions beyond scale in the specified infinitesimal sense.
