# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **3 points:** Uses symmetry of the RKHS unit ball to deduce from Δ(ρ)=0 that 𝔼ρ[𝒯g]=0 for every g∈ℋᵈ, and obtains 𝒯(∇u)=2ℒu for compactly supported smooth u.
- **2 points:** Turns the resulting generator identity on the applicable core into stationarity of ρ. A direct valid derivation of stationarity from zero current earns the same credit.
- **3 points:** Uses compactly supported vector tests or an equivalent justified argument to force the probability current or stream term to vanish, and establishes detailed balance and reversibility. Merely naming integration by parts, universality, or detailed balance without establishing the needed vanishing earns at most partial credit.
- **2 points:** Proves that stationarity plus reversibility gives zero current or an equivalent detailed-balance identity, and extends the resulting zero expectation to every RKHS test field, for example by a valid cutoff argument.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept any complete proof under the stated assumptions, including one using a justified stream-coefficient formulation instead of the probability current. A direct derivation of zero current may support both stationarity and reversibility steps. Do not require strict pointwise positivity of ρ, an L² condition on its score, or an additional unlisted decay hypothesis. Do not award downstream credit when current or stream-term elimination remains unsupported, but do award it when independently proved.
