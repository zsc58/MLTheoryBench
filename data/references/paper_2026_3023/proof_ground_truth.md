# Reference Proof

## Proof Structure

1. Fix a perturbation. Express its discounted reward using an independent exponential time, change to the nominal finite-horizon path law, and then apply Jensen. Derive the likelihood-ratio compensator and its discounted relative-entropy-rate contribution.
2. Bound the policy-averaged relative-entropy rate by the actionwise rates, apply reward Jensen, and rewrite the resulting integrand using the local cost c.
3. Prove the statewise Gibbs inequality, convert to normalized nominal occupancy with the factor 1/r, and apply uncertainty-set membership.
4. Exponentiate the bound for each perturbation and then take the infimum.

## Complete Proof

Fix the nominal parameter theta and an admissible stationary policy pi. All expectations below use initial law rho. Write d(s)=bar d_{rho,theta}^pi(s). The occupancy definition gives sum_s d(s)=1 and, for any real function g on S,

`E_theta^pi[int_0^infty exp(-rt)g(S_t)dt] = (1/r)sum_s d(s)g(s)`.

The nominal parameter itself belongs to the uncertainty set: its local cost is zero, so the constraint value is zero. Thus the uncertainty set is nonempty even when epsilon=0.

Fix tilde theta in C_{tau,epsilon}^pi(theta), and abbreviate

`R_tilde^pi(s) = sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)R_tilde theta(s,a)`,

`q(s,s') = sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)lambda_theta^a(s,s')`,

`q_tilde(s,s') = sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)lambda_tilde theta^a(s,s')`.

For this fixed model pair, finiteness of the spaces and of the rates makes both chains nonexplosive. Common actionwise support gives common support of q and q_tilde. Both chains start from rho. Strictly positive finite rewards imply that

`V_tilde = E_tilde theta^pi[int_0^infty exp(-rt)R_tilde^pi(S_t)dt]`

is finite and strictly positive. No positivity of the robust infimum is assumed at this stage.

### Likelihood Ratio and Nominal-Law Jensen

Let F_t be the sigma-algebra generated by the state path through time t. The finite-horizon path laws are mutually absolutely continuous. Under the nominal law their perturbed-to-nominal density ratio Z_t satisfies

`log Z_t = sum_{0<u<=t: S_u!=S_{u-}} log(q_tilde(S_{u-},S_u)/q(S_{u-},S_u)) - int_0^t sum_{s'!=S_u}(q_tilde(S_u,s')-q(S_u,s'))du`.

Indeed, the density of a specified finite-jump path is its initial probability times the product of its jump rates times the exponential of minus its integrated exit rate. The initial probabilities cancel because rho is unchanged. Transitions with two zero rates never occur and are omitted.

Define the policy-averaged local rate

`ell_pi(s) = sum_{s'!=s}[q(s,s')log(q(s,s')/q_tilde(s,s'))-q(s,s')+q_tilde(s,s')]`,

with zero contribution for a pair of zero rates. Under the nominal law, the transition count from s to s' has compensator

`int_0^t 1{S_{u-}=s}q(s,s')du`.

Taking expectations in the likelihood formula therefore gives

`E_theta^pi[log Z_t] = -E_theta^pi[int_0^t ell_pi(S_u)du]`.

For this fixed model pair, all log ratios on common positive support are bounded. The nominal total exit rate is bounded, so the expected number of jumps up to t is at most a finite constant times t. Consequently `E_theta^pi[|log Z_t|] <= K t` for some finite K depending on this model pair. All subsequent exponentially discounted integrals of this quantity are absolutely integrable. No bound uniform over the parameter family is needed.

Let T be independent of the chain with exponential rate r under either model. Finite-horizon change of measure and integration over T give

`r V_tilde = E_tilde theta^pi[R_tilde^pi(S_T)] = E_theta^pi[Z_T R_tilde^pi(S_T)]`.

The positive variable on the right is integrable, and its logarithm is integrable by the preceding likelihood estimate and boundedness of log R_tilde^pi. Jensen now applies under the nominal law:

`log V_tilde >= -log r + E_theta^pi[log Z_T] + E_theta^pi[log R_tilde^pi(S_T)]`.

The compensator identity and discounted Fubini yield

`E_theta^pi[log Z_T] = -int_0^infty r exp(-rt) E_theta^pi[int_0^t ell_pi(S_u)du]dt`

`= -E_theta^pi[int_0^infty exp(-ru)ell_pi(S_u)du]`,

because `int_u^infty r exp(-rt)dt=exp(-ru)`. The rate ell_pi is a bounded nonnegative function for this fixed pair, so this interchange is justified also by Tonelli.

At every state, concavity of log gives

`log R_tilde^pi(s) >= sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)log R_tilde theta(s,a)`.

Integration against the exponential-time density therefore gives

`log V_tilde >= -log r + E_theta^pi[int_0^infty exp(-rt)(sum_{a in A_{S_t}}pi(a|S_t)r log R_tilde theta(S_t,a)-ell_pi(S_t))dt]`.

### Action-Averaging Bound and Local Cost

For completeness, the action-averaging rate bound follows from the finite log-sum inequality. Fix s and s'!=s. Put `u_a=pi(a|s)lambda_theta^a(s,s')`, `v_a=pi(a|s)lambda_tilde theta^a(s,s')`, `U=sum_a u_a`, and `W=sum_a v_a`.

If U=W=0, all contributions vanish. Otherwise common support makes both U and W positive and gives identical support to the probability vectors `alpha_a=u_a/U` and `beta_a=v_a/W`. Expansion of their nonnegative relative entropy gives

`sum_a u_a log(u_a/v_a) - U log(U/W) = U KL(alpha||beta) >= 0`.

Zero u_a and v_a terms are omitted. On positive terms, u_a/v_a equals the corresponding nominal-to-perturbed actionwise rate ratio. Subtracting U and adding W, and then summing over destinations, proves

`ell_pi(s) <= sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)ell(lambda_theta^a,lambda_tilde theta^a)(s)`.

Thus

`log V_tilde >= -log r + E_theta^pi[int_0^infty exp(-rt)sum_{a in A_{S_t}}pi(a|S_t)(r log R_tilde theta(S_t,a)-ell(lambda_theta^a,lambda_tilde theta^a)(S_t))dt]`.

By the definition of c,

`r log R_tilde theta(s,a)-ell(lambda_theta^a,lambda_tilde theta^a)(s) = r log R_theta(s,a)-c_theta,tilde theta(s,a)`.

Set `c(s,a)=c_theta,tilde theta(s,a)`. The correct normalized-occupancy form is

`log V_tilde >= -log r + (1/r)sum_s d(s)[sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)r log R_theta(s,a)-sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)c(s,a)]`.

### Statewise Entropy Inequality and the Budget

For each state let

`Z_s = sum_{a in A_s}mu(a|s)exp(c(s,a)/tau)`,

`nu_s(a) = mu(a|s)exp(c(s,a)/tau)/Z_s`.

All costs are finite, so nu_s is a positive probability vector on A_s. Expanding relative entropy, with zero pi terms interpreted as zero, gives

`tau KL(pi(.|s)||nu_s) = tau KL(pi(.|s)||mu(.|s))-sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)c(s,a)+tau log Z_s`.

Nonnegativity follows, for example, by applying `log x<=x-1` on the support of pi: `KL(pi||nu_s)>=sum_{pi(a)>0}(pi(a)-nu_s(a))>=0`. Hence

`sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)c(s,a) <= tau KL(pi(.|s)||mu(.|s))+tau log Z_s`.

This argument does not require pi to have full support and does not assert attainment of a variational infimum.

Multiply by d(s), sum over states, and use tilde theta's uncertainty-set membership:

`sum_s d(s)sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)c(s,a) <= epsilon + sum_s d(s)tau KL(pi(.|s)||mu(.|s))`.

Substitution gives

`log V_tilde >= -log r + (1/r)sum_s d(s)[sum_{a in A_s}pi(a|s)r log R_theta(s,a)-tau KL(pi(.|s)||mu(.|s))]-epsilon/r`.

Using the occupancy identity once more,

`log V_tilde >= E_theta^pi[int_0^infty exp(-rt)(sum_{a in A_{S_t}}pi(a|S_t)r log R_theta(S_t,a)-tau KL(pi(.|S_t)||mu(.|S_t)))dt]-epsilon/r-log r`.

The right side is finite and independent of tilde theta. Exponentiate this inequality for each member of the uncertainty set and then take the infimum. This proves exactly the required robust lower bound and establishes positivity of the robust infimum as a conclusion. Zero occupancies, zero policy probabilities, absorbing states, and epsilon=0 are all included. No minimizing perturbed parameter is required.

## Proof-Obligation Closure

- The finite-horizon likelihood formula and its hypotheses are stated and verified; its elementary path-density derivation is included.
- The compensator expectation and the exponentially discounted Fubini step are explicitly derived with integrability verified for each fixed model pair.
- The action-averaging relative-entropy-rate inequality is derived, including zero-rate and zero-policy cases.
- The statewise Gibbs inequality is derived from a finite relative-entropy calculation without requiring variational attainment.
- The normalized-occupancy factor 1/r, the epsilon/r penalty, and the log r constant are preserved throughout.
- The robust infimum is taken only after a uniform pointwise lower bound has been established.

No unresolved theorem-critical proof obligation remains.
