# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **3 points:** Correctly identifies that the empirical scatter and prior-mean correction are positive semidefinite and deduces \(\Lambda_n\succeq\Lambda_0\succ0\), including the relevant posterior formulas.
- **2 points:** Verifies \(\kappa_n>0\), \(\nu_n-d+1>0\), and positivity of the scalar multiplier, thereby proving \(\Sigma_n\succ0\).
- **3 points:** Establishes from the multivariate Student-t density (or an equivalent valid argument) that the predictive distribution is proper and that its density, hence log-density, is finite for every embedding.
- **2 points:** Handles all required quantifiers and explicitly explains why no invertibility of the sample scatter is needed, so the conclusion includes \(K<d\).

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices.
