# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly defines the normalized weighted predictive, its finite positive normalizer, the per-observation ratio, and the running product under the stated filtration and predictive null.
- **3 points:** Conditions on the past and realized input and derives the nonnegative conditional mean-one identity by integrating against the source predictive.
- **2 points:** Uses measurability and the tower property to prove that the product process is a nonnegative martingale initialized at one.
- **3 points:** Applies Ville's inequality correctly to the infinite-horizon crossing rule and obtains the level-$\alpha$ bound for the stopping time.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. A proof must justify conditioning on potentially adaptive inputs and may not simply name an e-process or Ville's inequality without verifying the required hypotheses.
