# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Derives the exact ordinary and spectrally weighted OLS boosting recursions (or an equivalent singular-coordinate representation) and takes conditional expectations correctly.
- **4 points:** Decomposes the spectral conditional bias into top-band/bulk signal and confounding terms and proves all four normalized terms vanish using the energy, subspace-separation, weight-separation, and intermediate-stopping assumptions.
- **2 points:** Proves the ordinary-boosting formula and establishes a positive asymptotic lower bound on its normalized conditional bias under persistent confounding.
- **2 points:** States the combined asymptotic conclusions with the required quantifiers and limits, without adding assumptions or omitting either method's conclusion.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. A named method without the coordinate recursion and four-term limiting argument does not earn the central-bottleneck points.
