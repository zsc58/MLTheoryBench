# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **4 points:** Correctly applies the Radon–Nikodym change-of-measure identity to write conditional masking as the corresponding marginal integral weighted by \(w_{x_S}\). Merely mentioning change of measure without deriving the displayed identity does not earn full credit.
- **3 points:** Subtracts the marginal masking expression and correctly identifies \(m_{\mathrm c}(x,S)-m_{\mathrm m}(x,S)=\delta(x,S)\), hence \(v_{\mathrm c}-v_{\mathrm m}=d\) at \(x^0\).
- **3 points:** Establishes linearity of the selected attribution operator, or otherwise directly verifies the needed difference identity, and concludes \(\phi_{\mathrm c}-\phi_{\mathrm m}=I(d)\).

# Evaluator Notes

Accept any complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. A proof that obtains the masking discrepancy but does not justify its propagation through the attribution operator has not established the vector conclusion. Conversely, an independently correct direct verification for the selected operator is sufficient even if it does not explicitly use the word “linearity.”
