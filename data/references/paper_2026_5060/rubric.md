# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **4 points:** Correctly use the spectral decomposition and the change of variables \(u=\Sigma^{\beta/2}w\) to conjugate the generalized eigenproblem to \(\Sigma^{1-\beta}u=\lambda u\), with valid use of positive-definite matrix powers.
- **3 points:** Derive the full generalized eigenpair description: eigenvalues \(\lambda_i^{1-\beta}\) and corresponding directions \(w_i=\Sigma^{-\beta/2}v_i\), for all \(i\).
- **3 points:** Compute \(\kappa(\beta)=(\lambda_1/\lambda_p)^{1-\beta}\) and justify continuity, monotone nonincrease (strictness when anisotropic), and the endpoint values \(\kappa(0)=\lambda_1/\lambda_p\), \(\kappa(1)=1\).

# Evaluator Notes

Accept any complete valid alternative proof under the stated assumptions. Do not require the reference proof's notation or ordering. Treat failure to establish the conjugation/effective operator or the condition-number behavior as theorem-critical.
