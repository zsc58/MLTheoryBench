# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly expands \(F\) into \(z^\top\bar A z-2z^\top b+C\) and uses positive definiteness to identify \(u_H\) as its minimizer.
- **4 points:** Correctly evaluates the objective gap and completes the square to obtain \((\bar A\bar u-b)^\top\bar A^{-1}(\bar A\bar u-b)\).
- **3 points:** Derives \(c=b-\bar A\bar u\) from the normalized weighted-average identities.
- **1 point:** Substitutes this identity and correctly proves nonnegativity and the equality characterization using positive definiteness.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. A merely asserted completion-of-squares identity or correlation identity does not earn the corresponding derivation credit.
