# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly specializes the supplied one-dimensional ODE to the quadratic and quartic losses and justifies the symmetric branch m=0 relevant to the extent analysis.
- **2 points:** Derives the quadratic extent evolution and correctly proves the attracting, unstable, and neutral regimes separated by S=2/eta along that branch.
- **3 points:** Derives the quartic extent dynamics on the symmetric branch and obtains the positive equilibrium Sigma_* = -(S-2/eta)/Q in the regime S>2/eta, Q<0.
- **3 points:** Correctly analyzes local stability of the zero-extent state and Sigma_* for the induced scalar extent dynamics on the symmetric branch, including positivity and finite-amplitude self-stabilization above threshold.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept any complete valid alternative proof under the public assumptions. The quartic stability score concerns the induced one-dimensional extent dynamics restricted to m=0, not transverse stability of the full two-dimensional state. Do not require the reference proof's notation or ordering, and do not award credit merely for naming a method.
