# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **3 points:** Establishes that \(K_u\) determines all probe values and proves the converse: equality against every PSD \(C\) forces \(K_f=K_g\), for example by rank-one PSD probes. This must justify the stated minimal-complete interpretation.
- **4 points:** Proves the central spectral comparison. It verifies that the lifted matrices are SPD, bounds every eigenvalue of \(H_f^{-1/2}H_gH_f^{-1/2}\) from the log-spectrum distance, and correctly derives \(e^{-d}H_f\preceq H_g\preceq e^dH_f\).
- **3 points:** Transfers the Loewner sandwich to arbitrary PSD probes using congruence and trace monotonicity, and correctly rewrites \(d=\sqrt{k}\,\delta\) to obtain the two claimed powers of \(R\).

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. A mere assertion that PSD probes separate matrices, or that a Riemannian distance gives the required Loewner bounds, does not earn the corresponding full credit without a derivation.
