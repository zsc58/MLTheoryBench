# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Expresses each coordinate bridge as a binomial mass on the monotone finite path, differentiates it correctly for interior times, handles both support boundaries and the t=0 endpoint directly, and notes the equal-endpoint case.
- **3 points:** For an increasing endpoint, derives the adjacent-mass ratio and verifies the birth-only forward equation with rate (b_i-z_i)_+/(1-t), including support-boundary and off-support states.
- **3 points:** For a decreasing endpoint, derives the analogous ratio and verifies the death-only forward equation with rate (z_i-b_i)_+/(1-t), including support-boundary and off-support states and the equal-endpoint case.
- **2 points:** Combines coordinates via the product law, identifies the finite invariant reachable box, invokes uniqueness of the time-inhomogeneous forward equation under the stated rates, and concludes exact bridge generation including the limiting endpoint behavior.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. Award full credit only when endpoint-time and support-boundary issues are handled either directly or by a clearly stated equivalent argument. Do not award credit for merely naming a birth-death or flow-matching method without deriving the rates and checking the forward equation.
