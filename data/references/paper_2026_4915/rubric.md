# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

1. **2 points:** Correctly identifies the structural set I, its size D, the total length T, the two normalized estimators, and the audited mean/variance/independence assumptions.
2. **3 points:** Derives the token-level expectation D mu/T and variance sigma^2/T.
3. **3 points:** Derives the option-level expectation mu and variance sigma^2/D.
4. **2 points:** Applies the SNR definition and obtains the ratio sqrt(T/D), with valid positivity conditions.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept any complete valid alternative proof. Do not award credit for merely naming a variance-reduction method without deriving the required moments and ratio.
