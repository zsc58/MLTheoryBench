# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **3 points:** Derives the retained-law mixture and one-sided transfer bound, then obtains clean coverage for every qualifying retained-score threshold.
- **3 points:** Uses the clean trimming covariance identity to prove \(\Delta=\rho/r\) when \(p_c=p_d=r>0\), and correctly relates clean-side covariance distortion to score-neutral retention. A separately displayed one-sided supremum identity is not required.
- **2 points:** Establishes the quantitative separation bound for \(\lambda\geq0\), its vanishing-ratio consequence with \(\varepsilon<1\) fixed, and the equal-retention identity, including zero-contamination edge cases.
- **2 points:** Derives the covariance-refined no-separation bound and explains why equal retention probabilities cannot improve the contamination coefficient.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. Assess the stated target under \(p_c>0\); do not require an extension to \(p_c=0\). A proof need not establish a separate one-sided covariance supremum formula unless it uses that formula. Treat omission of the requested covariance or no-separation conclusion as a theorem-critical gap.
