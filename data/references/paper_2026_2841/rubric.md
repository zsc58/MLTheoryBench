# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly identifies the retained-correct and retained-error probabilities as \(pS_1(\tau)\) and \((1-p)S_0(\tau)\), and notes the positive-yield condition.
- **3 points:** Applies Bayes' rule and derives the exact identity \(A(\tau)-p= p(1-p)D(\tau)/[S_1(\tau)-(1-p)D(\tau)]\), including valid denominator algebra.
- **3 points:** Uses \(0<p<1\) and denominator positivity to prove strict improvement when \(D(\tau)>0\), and non-improvement when \(D(\tau)\le0\).
- **2 points:** Establishes the equality characterization \(A(\tau)=p\iff D(\tau)=0\) (under positive yield) and the resulting statement for all thresholds.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. Do not award credit for merely naming Bayes' rule or score separability without carrying out the probability decomposition and sign argument.
