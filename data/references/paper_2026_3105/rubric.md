# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **3 points:** Correctly derives both column identities, including the diagonally scaled identity, from the partition-matrix entries and group indicators; explicitly uses $Z_{jj}=1/|C_{k(j)}|$.
- **4 points:** Proves both directions of the proportional-representation equivalence for every group and cluster, including the passage between all columns and all nonempty clusters.
- **3 points:** Proves both directions of the tau-ratio equivalence, correctly handling the positive factors $Z_{jj}$ and $|P_g|$, and identifies the resulting inequalities as linear in the partition-matrix variables.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices.
