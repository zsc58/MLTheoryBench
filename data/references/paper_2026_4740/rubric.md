# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly handles the exponential reparameterization and integral potential and justifies well-definedness and differentiation using the stated regularity on K.
- **4 points:** Proves the exponential-path lower bound under the task's assumptions, including the zero-path case, and combines it with the DR-submodular increment inequality to control the comparator term. Merely naming an auxiliary result or specialized method without closing its applicable hypotheses and lower-bound obligation earns no derivation credit.
- **2 points:** Computes the surrogate gradient and evaluates the negative trajectory pairing by integration by parts, including the nonnegative boundary contribution.
- **2 points:** Adds the bounds, cancels the integral terms, and concludes the exact 1/e-linearizability inequality with the required h, beta, and surrogate.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept any complete valid alternative proof under the stated assumptions. Do not require the reference proof's presentation choices.
