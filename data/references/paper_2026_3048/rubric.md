# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Derives the normal-scale-mixture tail asymptotic and the required compact-uniform local density-ratio limit from regular variation.
- **3 points:** Uses the centered posterior cumulant/Tweedie identity with the sample-mean variance to prove Gaussian concentration of theta minus the residual mean, with the correct limiting variance.
- **2 points:** Computes the fixed-variance posterior-predictive limit and proves that its negative Gaussian form is strictly increasing in \(|\Delta|\), establishing centered-residual-order equivalence.
- **3 points:** Defines the empirical within-residual variance in the plug-in construction, derives its augmented identity \(\widehat v_m^2+\Delta^2/(n+1)\), handles the resulting varying variance uniformly, obtains the stated \(h_{n,v}\) limit, and proves its strict monotonicity.
