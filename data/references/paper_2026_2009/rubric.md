# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly constructs and justifies an open dense set of positive probability-density tuples whose coordinate-ratio map is an embedding, including the Whitney dimension condition and normalization argument. Establishes relative openness separately from density; does not infer that a continuous surjection preserves openness.
- **3 points:** Proves pushforward identifiability by writing the change-of-variables densities relative to the fixed Riemannian volumes, cancelling the common Jacobian through ratios, and using injectivity to conclude f=g. Correctly interprets equality as equality of measures and justifies its pointwise use, for example by continuity and full support of volume.
- **4 points:** Proves weighted-divergence identifiability by expanding ρ_j^{-1}div(ρ_j u), subtracting the normalized reference equation, obtaining the differential of the logarithmic-ratio map, and using its injectivity to conclude v=w.
- **1 point:** Keeps all arguments under the stated smooth-manifold, C^1-function, C^1-map, C^1-vector-field, positivity, compactness, Riemannian-volume, and parameter assumptions. Uses the same generic set for both conclusions and all universally quantified maps and fields. Does not require nonexistent diffeomorphisms or divide by a zero normalization integral in an empty-space case.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. Award no credit for merely naming Whitney embeddings, Jacobian cancellation, or logarithmic ratios without deriving their role.

The standard Whitney generic embedding theorem may be cited without proof, provided its applicable open-dense C^1 statement and dimension requirement are correctly used. Other core construction and recovery steps must be justified rather than replaced by citations to paper-specific lemmas.

A C^1 diffeomorphism gives continuous pushforward densities here; do not require or assume that those densities are C^1. A routine omitted almost-everywhere-to-pointwise justification or vacuous case is a locally repairable omission, not grounds to reject an otherwise essentially complete proof. In dimension zero, differentials act on zero tangent spaces; no extra positive-dimension restriction is needed. Neither connectedness nor orientability is required.
