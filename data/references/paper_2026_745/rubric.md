# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly differentiates the entropy through the VP Fokker-Planck equation and obtains the Fisher-score difference, with cancellation of the common drift contribution.
- **3 points:** Derives the exact pairwise identity using the mixture-score formula and weighted variance, including the correct factor and conditional expectations.
- **3 points:** Computes the isotropic Gaussian score gaps and posterior log-odds, showing the Gaussian law of Lambda_rs and the reduced lambda_rs-weighted expression for entropy production.
- **2 points:** Proves the three-regime VP asymptotics: lambda_rs comparable to d exp(-2t), exponential suppression for lambda to infinity, linear suppression for lambda to zero, and the resulting O(1) critical window around (1/2) log d.
