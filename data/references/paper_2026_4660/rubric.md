# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **3 points:** Correctly defines the realized trajectory velocity, endpoint displacement velocity, and deviation, and proves that the deviation has zero temporal mean from the flow endpoint identity.
- **2 points:** Uses the stated regularity to justify that the deviation is an H^1 function and that its derivative equals the trajectory acceleration.
- **3 points:** Correctly applies the zero-mean Poincare-Wirtinger inequality with constant 1/pi^2 to relate deviation energy to integrated squared derivative.
- **2 points:** Combines the positive excess-energy premise with the inequality to derive kappa >= pi^2 Delta_local > 0 for the off-manifold trajectory.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. Treat omission of the zero-mean justification, the H^1 applicability, or the pi^2 constant as theorem-critical gaps.
