# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **3 points:** Correctly expands each procedure's finite-horizon error into stationary risk plus transient excess and derives the taskwise identity \(g_N^k=-\iota^k+\varepsilon_N^k(S)-\varepsilon_N^k(R)\).
- **3 points:** Averages the taskwise identity correctly and, for common duration \(N\), converts transient excesses to cumulative transient quantities to obtain \(\bar g_N=-\bar\iota+D_N/N\).
- **3 points:** Uses \(\bar\iota>0\) and the individual sublinearity of both averaged cumulative transient quantities to establish \(D_N=o(N)\) and then prove \(\bar g_N<0\) for all sufficiently large \(N\).
- **1 point:** Correctly derives the breakeven relation \(N_{\mathrm{crit}}=D_{N_{\mathrm{crit}}}/\bar\iota\), including that the horizon-dependent cumulative terms are evaluated at the breakeven scale.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. A response that treats \(D_N\) as independent of \(N\) without qualification does not fully establish the critical-duration clause.
