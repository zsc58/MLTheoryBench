# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **3 points:** Correctly expands both quadratic forms using the zero diagonal and simplex constraint, and proves the weighted ordered-pair average representation with the required nondegeneracy.
- **3 points:** Proves the normalization characterization: the complete-network value-one condition forces \(D(w)=w^T(11^T-I)w=1-\sum_iw_i^2\) for every admissible w, including the uniqueness quantifier.
- **4 points:** Derives the density-times-assortativity identity by regrouping linked and all ordered-pair sums, with correct definitions and positivity conditions.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. Treat omission of the uniqueness argument or the density-assortativity factorization as a theorem-critical gap.
