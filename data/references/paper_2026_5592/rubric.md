# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

1. **2 points:** Defines a valid epoch-local prediction rule and relaxation, proves the probability lies in [0,1], and establishes the one-step exponential admissibility inequality. The logarithm and exponential must have consistent bases. Under the reference route, the potential uses predictable learner loss, not uncorrected realized randomized loss.
2. **3 points:** Establishes the supermartingale and entropy transfer with the correct divergence direction, bounds the initial relaxation using the uniform VC Rademacher estimate and concentration conditional on the covariates, and obtains the ideal conditional per-epoch bound. No independence of the actual covariates may be assumed. Expectations over randomized prediction must be justified.
3. **2 points:** Supplies a genuine conditional-rollout implementation using the stated ordinary agnostic ERM oracle and efficiently evaluable hypotheses. Proves the paired exponential-moment ratio estimate, or an equally valid alternative, with sufficient control to use polynomially many samples. Under the reference route, shared samples yield the eta cancellation, historical comparator-loss offsets cancel in the normalized variance, and N proportional to the epoch length suffices. Rollouts stop at the epoch boundary while conditioning retains the full observed prefix.
4. **1 point:** Compares global and epoch regrets, applies the conditional KL chain rule, and supplies a justified aggregation or adaptation procedure not requiring knowledge of Q, g, or KL. Obtains the stated rate without an unabsorbed logarithmic factor. Handles unequal epoch lengths and the trivial large-divergence and zero-dimensional cases. The reference's elementary multi-rate weights proof is one acceptable implementation; its particular prior and rates are not mandatory.
5. **2 points:** Gives a valid threshold converse in the same information model with a fixed oblivious labeling function and exact binary KL equal to D. Establishes comparator realizability on distinct block points, fresh-label unpredictability despite simulator access, a square-root block-majority advantage, and learner-first probabilistic-method extraction. Accounts for nondivisible horizons and D=1. In a D-block tree construction there are only D-1 observable transitions; the final comparator branch must not be counted as an additional covariate observation. The reference's disjoint-support simulator mixture repairs this count, but any correct exact-KL construction is acceptable.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices, node coordinates, simulator mixture, or named algorithms. Standard independent probability inequalities and the uniform VC Rademacher bound may be invoked with their relevant hypotheses. A specialized algorithm name or source-paper theorem citation alone does not establish the needed guarantee.

A proof using natural-log relative entropy may convert it to the public binary-KL convention; universal constants may absorb ln 2 in upper bounds, but the converse must attain the stated exact divergence after conversion.

Do not credit the asserted supermartingale merely because it resembles the source if the proof substitutes realized loss for predictable loss without a valid correction. Propagate a missing supermartingale or entropy-transfer argument to the final upper-bound score. Do not award full implementation credit for an unsupported polynomial-sampling assertion or for granting a stronger optimization oracle.

Do not award full converse credit for assigning probability 2^{-D} to a D-block trajectory generated by only D-1 fair transitions, for assigning independent labels to repeated identical covariates, or for a simulator that reveals the supposedly fresh labels. A valid majority calculation and an information-compatible construction are both necessary.

The reference proof has no unresolved private obligations. All five core steps are supported by the replacement derivations and explicitly stated standard independent background.
