# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly formulate the coordinatewise shifted variables modulo one and justify that torus translation preserves each uniform marginal and joint independence.
- **2 points:** Correctly identify the two affine branches of the activation and relate the real-valued activation to its modulo-one representative.
- **4 points:** For an arbitrary interval in `[0,1]`, compute both preimage intervals under the triangle wave, show their total length equals the interval length, and conclude the scalar pushforward is uniform (with a valid extension from intervals to the Borel law).
- **2 points:** Lift the scalar result through the independent coordinate maps and conclude the full output vector is uniform on the hypercube.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. Deduct for treating measure preservation as an unsupported assertion or for omitting the independence argument needed for the joint law.
