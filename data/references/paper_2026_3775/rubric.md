# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly formulates policy feasibility as C(x)p=f(x) with p\ge0, including the Bellman-dependent coefficient matrix and row-sum constraints, and identifies the unique discounted value interpretation.
- **3 points:** Performs rank stratification and proves the minor conditions characterize rank C(x)=\rho and consistency rank [C(x)|f(x)]=\rho.
- **3 points:** Establishes basic-feasible support reduction for a nonnegative solution, including the nullspace perturbation argument and extension to an invertible \rho-column basis.
- **2 points:** Applies Cramer's rule to obtain determinant-product sign inequalities and proves both inclusions, yielding the finite quantifier-free basic semi-algebraic union.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. The support-reduction and determinant-sign argument is the characteristic bottleneck; merely naming linear programming, rank stratification, or Cramer's rule without carrying out the implications is insufficient. Deduct for omitted consistency conditions, failure to justify nonnegative basic support, or proving only one inclusion.
