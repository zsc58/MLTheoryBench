# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly sets up the lifted distance and applies the C1 first-order expansion of the scaling map, obtaining the one-sided local quadratic metric with an o(||h||^2) remainder.
- **3 points:** Correctly exchanges the endpoints and averages the two expansions to derive the symmetric tensor \(\bar G(x,x')\) appearing in the target.
- **3 points:** Justifies passage from squared distance to distance, including the local SPD lower bound and control of the square-root remainder.
- **2 points:** Uses continuity of the radial profile at zero to transfer the distance expansion to the covariance and retains the nugget term exactly.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. Naming Taylor expansion, symmetrization, or continuity without carrying out the corresponding estimates does not earn the associated points. Propagate a missing metric derivation to the final covariance conclusion when it leaves that conclusion unsupported.
