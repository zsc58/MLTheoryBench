# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

1. **Entropic dual/Gibbs foundation (2 points):** Establish existence and uniqueness of the entropic optimizer on compact supports, choose the centered continuous Schrödinger potential, and state its Gibbs factorization and semidual optimality. A standard entropic duality theorem may supply existence, dual attainment, and Gibbs factorization when its relevant statement and hypotheses are given and checked; justify the continuous pointwise representatives.
2. **Centered feature-class approximation (3 points):** Prove that injectivity and compactness transfer uniform density to the pullback class, center the approximants, and obtain uniform convergence to the normalized target potential.
3. **Semidual convergence via transform stability (2 points):** Derive the 1-Lipschitz sup-norm bound for the soft transform and use it to prove convergence of transformed potentials and semidual values to the optimum.
4. **Plan recovery (3 points):** Define the induced Gibbs plans, prove the exact reverse-KL gap identity, then deduce KL and total-variation convergence (with the correct Pinsker constant) and weak convergence.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. A method name alone, without either a derivation or the relevant statement and verified hypotheses of an applicable standard independent result, does not complete a step. Treat failure to establish the gap identity or convergence as a theorem-critical omission.
