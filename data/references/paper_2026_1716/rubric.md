# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

1. **[2 points]** Correctly express the complex density in polar coordinates, evaluate the angular integral with the modified-Bessel identity, and derive the amplitude density with the correct parameter domain.
2. **[2 points]** Establish the normalizing constant by a valid nonnegative series/gamma-integral or equivalent argument, identifying the Laguerre/confluent-hypergeometric relation; do not merely assert normalization.
3. **[2 points]** Derive the specializations a=1 as Rice and the centered case as Nakagami-m, with Rayleigh and one-sided-normal subcases correctly identified.
4. **[3 points]** Perform the X=R^2 transformation with beta and lambda, derive the power density, and obtain the gamma-mixture weights by expanding I_0 and regrouping terms. This is the characteristic bottleneck; the Jacobian, powers, rates, and normalizer must be correct.
5. **[1 point]** Prove the mixture weights are nonnegative and sum to one, including the lambda=0 gamma limit, or give an equivalent complete normalization argument for the power representation.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. Deduct for theorem-critical errors in exponents, Jacobians, parameter mappings, or normalization even when the overall architecture is recognizable.
