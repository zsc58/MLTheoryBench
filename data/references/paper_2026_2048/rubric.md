# Scoring Guide

- **0:** No correct idea or relevant valid method.
- **1-2:** Nontrivial relevant ideas, but no complete correct proof step or useful conclusion.
- **3-7:** Some correct core steps or conclusions, but important steps are missing.
- **8-9:** Complete and essentially correct, with only readily repairable errors or omitted cases.
- **10:** Fully correct.

# Core Steps

- **2 points:** Correctly establishes the Hermite-square normalization S=1+sum_alpha alpha! c_alpha^2 and identifies the lower-orthant integral of the density.
- **2 points:** Expands the square and evaluates the Gaussian and linear Hermite contributions using coordinate factorization and the integrated-Hermite functions G_n.
- **4 points:** Derives the quadratic contribution for all coordinate pairs by applying the Hermite product identity, integrating each term, and obtaining the finite J_{p,q} expression, including cross-index terms.
- **2 points:** Combines the three contributions with the factor S^{-1} to obtain the stated CDF for every z and correctly notes the resulting direct probability/quantile evaluation.

# Evaluator Notes

Accept a complete valid alternative proof. Do not require the reference proof's incidental presentation choices. A proof that merely names Hermite identities without carrying out the coordinatewise expansion and J_{p,q} construction does not earn the quadratic-step weight.
