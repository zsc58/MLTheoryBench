# HKU Summer Internship Presentation Guide

本文档用于准备一次校内线上英文汇报：10 分钟 presentation，加 5 分钟 Q&A。项目暂用名为 **PaperProof: A Paper-Level Benchmark for Closed-Book ML-Theory Proof Reconstruction**。

当前实验仍在运行，因此下文数字采用 2026-08-23 20:14 的快照。最终汇报前只需替换标有 `[UPDATE]` 的数字和图表，不需要改变主线。

## 1. 汇报要讲清楚什么

十分钟不适合逐步讲解完整工程。听众离场时应记住四件事：

1. **Problem**：我们希望评估 LLM 和 agent 解决 theoretical machine learning（TML）问题的能力，但传统数学 benchmark 无法直接覆盖 TML 论文的假设组织、贡献结构和原文可靠性问题。
2. **Method**：我们把 2026 年论文的代表性理论贡献编译成匿名、闭卷、论文级 proof task，并用独立 reviewer 和 evaluator 评测。
3. **Evidence**：当前系统已经规模化运行；任务预估复杂度与 solver 实际得分呈明显单调关系，source audit 还产生了论文问题统计这一附加结果。
4. **Contribution**：本项目建立了一条可复现、重视 anti-leakage、source validity 和错误归属的完整评测链路，并为后续组内 agent 实验准备统一任务与评分接口。

推荐使用一句中心论点贯穿全场：

> PaperProof turns recent ML-theory papers into source-grounded, closed-book proof tasks and evaluates not only whether a model succeeds, but also how hard the reconstruction is and where failures come from.

不要把汇报讲成“我调用了很多 agent”。多 agent 是实现手段；研究贡献是可靠地构造和评测论文级证明任务。

## 2. 建议的 PPT 结构

建议准备 **11 张主幻灯片**，另附 3–5 张备用页。目标讲述时间为 9 分 45 秒左右，给线上延迟和临场停顿留出少量余量。Slide 6 和 Slide 8 是很短的过渡页，实际内容密度仍相当于约 9 张常规幻灯片。

### Slide 2. Why TML Needs a Different Benchmark: 80 seconds

**Slide message**：我们的目标是评估 LLM 和 agent 解决 TML 问题的能力，但传统数学 benchmark 不能直接照搬。

建议采用左右两栏对照：

| Many existing math benchmarks | Theoretical ML papers |
|---|---|
| Assumptions are usually explicit and self-contained. | Assumptions and standing conventions may be distributed across the paper and require reconstruction. |
| One clearly posed theorem or problem often forms the evaluation unit. | A contribution often consists of a chain of guarantees; one theorem may not represent it. |
| The problem statement is normally treated as fixed and rigorous. | Papers may prioritize the meaning of a guarantee, strengthen assumptions along the way, or contain informalities, omissions, and occasional errors. |

**What to say**

> Our long-term goal is to evaluate both language models and agents on theoretical machine-learning problems. The research group already has an agent for working on ML open problems, so once the benchmark is ready, that system can be evaluated on the same tasks.
>
> However, existing mathematical benchmarks cannot be transferred directly. In many mathematics benchmarks, assumptions are explicit and self-contained, and one clearly posed theorem forms the unit of contribution. In theoretical ML, assumptions and definitions are often distributed across the paper, while the main contribution may be a progression of several guarantees rather than one isolated theorem. Moreover, ML papers often emphasize the meaning and rate of the conclusion, and the source statement or proof may contain informal compression, strengthened assumptions, omissions, or genuine errors. Therefore, task construction and source auditing are themselves part of the benchmark design.

结尾抛出问题：

> How can we turn recent TML papers into faithful, closed-book, and scorable research-level proof tasks?

不要把纯数学与 TML 说成绝对对立。使用 `many existing benchmarks` 和 `often`，避免把领域差异过度概括。

### Slide 4. Thread A: From a Paper to a Flagship Chain: 90 seconds

**One-sentence summary**

> Thread A reads the full paper, reconstructs its theoretical contribution, selects a representative theorem chain, and audits the source mathematics.

页面主体用三个连续方框：

**Turn A1: Contribution Triage**

- Recover the paper's principal contribution and its theoretical-material map.
- Filter papers whose theory is not sufficiently substantive or representative.

**Turn A2: Flagship Target Selection**

- Build plausible chains from contribution-level theorem candidates.
- Score and rank chains for contribution fidelity and retained proof difficulty, without using theorem correctness as a selection signal.

**Turn A3: Source Recovery and Audit**

- Recover the full statements, assumptions, dependencies, and proofs from the paper.
- Label each result as clean, locally repairable, or containing a major source issue.

**What to say**

> Thread A is the paper-facing part of the pipeline. In the first turn, it reconstructs the broad contribution and maps candidate theoretical results, internal support, definitions, and external dependencies. It also filters papers whose theory is too weak or not representative enough for a paper-level benchmark.
>
> In the second turn, it constructs plausible flagship chains from the contribution-level candidates. Each chain is scored for how faithfully it represents the paper's theoretical contribution and, among equally faithful chains, how much characteristic proof difficulty it retains. Importantly, target selection is correctness-blind, so a mathematically problematic theorem is not silently avoided merely because it is difficult to validate.
>
> In the third turn, the model returns to the source and reconstructs the full statements and proofs. Issues are recorded for all candidates, but an issue and a pipeline stop are not identical: an issue outside the selected proof closure remains a side product, while the task stops only if every eligible flagship chain is blocked.

### Slide 5. Thread B: From a Chain to a Benchmark Task: 65 seconds

**One-sentence summary**

> Thread B cannot read the full paper; it converts the audited flagship material into a self-contained public task and private evaluation artifacts.

页面主体同样用三个连续方框：

**Turn B1: Formalization Compile**

- Produce the anonymized public task, complete private reference proof, provenance, and `rubric.md`.
- The private rubric totals 10 points and contains 2–5 weighted mathematical core steps.

**Turn B2: Artifact Audit**

- Check self-containment, source fidelity, mathematical validity, scope, scorability, and anti-leakage.
- Repair formalization defects or request one local source check when needed.

**Turn B3: PRC Assessment**

- Estimate Proof Reconstruction Complexity before seeing any solver answer.
- Separately detect whether the public statement exposes too much of the proof route.

**What to say**

> Thread B is source-blind. It receives only the audited flagship material. The first turn compiles one integrated public problem, a complete private reference proof, provenance, and a ten-point rubric containing two to five weighted mathematical core steps. The second turn adversarially checks whether these artifacts are self-contained, faithful, valid, scorable, and free of leakage. The third turn assigns a pre-solution Proof Reconstruction Complexity level and checks whether the task accidentally reveals the proof route.

### Slide 6. Closed-Book Solver: 30 seconds

页面建议只放一个 solver 框和四个醒目的限制：

- One model turn
- No tools, network, or retrieval
- No paper identity or private artifacts
- Partial progress is explicitly encouraged

**What to say**

> The baseline solver is a one-turn language-model call with no tools, network, retrieval, or paper identity. Standard background is allowed, but every non-elementary black-box theorem must be stated in an applicable form and its hypotheses checked. If uncertain, the solver should prove the result or mark a gap. Correct partial progress is explicitly encouraged.

### Slide 7. Independent Review and Scoring: 50 seconds

页面使用两个并列方框：

**Validity Reviewer**

- Reads only the public task and solver proof.
- Checks mathematical validity and identifies minor or major gaps.
- Does not see the reference proof or assign a score.

**Reference Evaluator**

- Reads the reviewer report, private proof, and rubric.
- Assigns an integer score from 0 to 10 with dependency-sensitive partial credit.
- Accepts correct alternative proof routes and attributes artifact errors separately.

**What to say**

> The evaluation design adapts ideas from a prompt used by a senior member of our group to review proposed solutions to ML open problems. First, a reference-blind validity reviewer judges the solver proof on its own mathematical merits and does not assign a score. Then a reference evaluator uses that report together with the private proof and rubric to award dependency-sensitive partial credit from zero to ten. A valid alternative proof can still receive full credit.

### Slide 8. Full Workflow and Revision Paths: 30 seconds

这一页使用完整工作流图，不再逐框解释。用动画或高亮依次显示主路径和两种回退：

1. Artifact or downstream objection returns to Thread B for bounded revision.
2. A possible source-level defect triggers a local source check or source re-audit in Thread A.

**What to say**

> This is the complete workflow. Objections return to the component with the right information: formalization defects go to Thread B, while source-level questions return to Thread A. Solver and reviewer feedback can trigger at most two validation revisions, preventing uncontrolled self-repair loops.

不要在主汇报中讲 MinerU、HTML、TeX 优先级或虚拟机命令；这些放备用页。

|---|---:|---:|

| PRC1 | 309 | 9.55 |
| PRC2 | 242 | 8.45 |
| PRC3 | 139 | 6.45 |

表下用一行列出 suitability 映射：

> PRC0: reject · PRC1: baseline · PRC2–3: primary · PRC4: stress

PRC 的五个评价维度放在表格上方或右下角，不展开 0–3 的逐档细则：

- **S**: Strategy Discovery
- **D**: Subgoal Structure and Dependency
- **T**: Technical Reasoning and Execution
- **C**: Capability Integration
- **E**: Proof-Route Exposure

**What to say**

> At this snapshot, 951 papers have produced complete and scored tasks, while other papers were filtered or stopped by quality controls. Before any solver answer is visible, we score four intrinsic dimensions: strategy discovery, dependency structure, technical execution, and capability integration. We separately check proof-route exposure. These scores define PRC0 to PRC4 and the corresponding reject, baseline, primary, and stress subsets.
>
> The most important current result is the monotonic separation in actual solver performance. Mean score decreases from 9.91 at PRC0 to 4.19 at PRC4. This suggests that the pre-solution assessment captures meaningful proof-reconstruction complexity, although cross-model validation is still needed.

### Slide 10. Source-Issue Analysis as a Side Product: 55 seconds

这里必须区分 pipeline state 和 paper-quality mapping。纵向使用 `clean / issue / suspect`，不要写 `clean / stop / suspect`，因为 `stop` 已经是横向流程状态。

左侧放交叉表：

| Paper-quality mapping | Completed | Stopped | Total | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `clean` | 621 | 0 | 621 | 33.1% |
| `issue` | 330 | 612 | 942 | 50.2% |
| `suspect` | 0 | 313 | 313 | 16.7% |
| **Total** | **951** | **925** | **1,876** | **100%** |

右侧用三层概括 Thread A Turn 3 的 source-audit 标准：

- **Clean**: the theorem closes under the recovered source contract, allowing normal research-paper compression.
- **Minor**: a local, source-grounded, claim-preserving repair is available.
- **Major**: the statement requires substantive alteration, or a theorem-critical proof gap remains after serious reconstruction.

At the aggregate level, `clean` includes source-clean results and allowable minor cases after their local source-grounded repairs. Major issues in candidates outside the final unblocked chain are recorded but may be excluded from the public task.

**What to say**

> Source auditing also gives us a useful side product: a structured record of possible issues in recent TML papers. Minor issues are allowed when they have a unique local repair, and normal research-paper compression is treated as clean. Major labels require a substantive statement problem or an unresolved theorem-critical gap.
>
> We manually audited a 50-paper calibration sample, revised the language and thresholds, and reran the pipeline. More than 95 percent of the resulting coarse classifications agreed with our human expectations. This supports the scale of the major-issue criterion, but it is a calibration result rather than a claim that every individual issue label is correct.

解释交叉表时强调：`issue` 不等于 `stopped`。某篇论文可以存在有问题的非选中 candidate，同时仍由另一条无阻断问题的 flagship chain 生成完整任务。`suspect` 表示多轮验证或最终 evaluator 仍无法稳定解决，不等同于确认原论文错误。

## 4. PPT 视觉设计

### 4.1 总体原则

- 使用 16:9，白底或非常浅的灰底，正文深灰，强调色最多两种。
- 标题建议 30–36 pt，正文 20–24 pt，图表标签至少 18 pt。
- 每页只表达一个结论；页标题尽量写成结论而非栏目名。例如用 `PRC strongly separates solver performance`，而不是 `Results`。
- 每页正文不超过 35–45 个英文单词，复杂解释放在口头讲述中。
- Slide 4 和 Slide 5 分别使用三步简图；Slide 8 再展示完整工作流。完整图应占据大部分页面，不要配长段正文。
- 图表直接标注样本数和关键数值，不依赖听众阅读图例。

### 4.2 推荐图表

主汇报建议使用以下核心视觉：

1. **TML versus conventional math benchmarks**：Slide 2 的三行对照表。
2. **Thread A and Thread B diagrams**：分别展示两个三-turn 结构，保持相同视觉语法。
3. **Full workflow diagram**：五个线程、正常主路径和条件回退。
4. **Outcome plus PRC table/chart**：Slide 9 左侧是状态小表，右侧用折线图或柱状图突出 PRC 与均分的单调关系。
5. **Source-issue matrix**：Slide 10 使用 completed/stopped 与 clean/issue/suspect 的交叉表。

Slide 9 的 PRC 图应当是全场最醒目的数据视觉。五个维度可以放成图表上方的一排短标签，不要与主数据争夺空间。

### 4.3 英文排版

- 使用短语，不使用完整论文段落。
- 统一采用 `paper-level`, `closed-book`, `source-grounded`, `reference-blind`。
- `proof reconstruction complexity` 首次写全称并标记 `PRC`，之后只用 PRC。
- `filtered out`、`stopped` 和 `completed` 是 pipeline states；`clean`、`issue` 和 `suspect` 是 paper-quality mappings，不要混用。
- 不把 `issue` 写成 `the paper is wrong`；建议写 `a source-level issue was detected or recorded`。

## 6. 英文讲述方式

### 6.1 用 signposting 帮助听众

推荐句型：

- `I will first explain the evaluation problem, then introduce our pipeline, and finally show the current results.`
- `The key design choice here is ...`
- `There are two points to notice from this figure.`
- `This result should be interpreted cautiously because ...`
- `The main takeaway is ...`

### 6.2 避免的表达

- 不说 `the model understands the paper`，改为 `the model reconstructs a proof under the task specification`。
- 不说 `our evaluator guarantees correctness`，改为 `the evaluator combines reference-blind review with GT-assisted scoring`。
- 不说 `50 percent of papers are wrong`，改为 `50 percent of formalized outcomes currently carry an issue label under our conservative automated audit`。
- 不说 `PRC is the true difficulty`，改为 `PRC is a pre-solution complexity estimate`。
- 不频繁使用 `basically`, `obviously`, `simply`；研究汇报中这些词容易弱化严谨性。

### 6.3 不要全文背稿

每页 speaker notes 只记三项：

1. 本页唯一结论；
2. 必须说出的一个数字或定义；
3. 连接下一页的一句话。

例如 Slide 9 的备注可以只有：

```text
Claim: PRC separates actual solver performance.
Numbers: 9.91 → 4.19 from PRC0 to PRC4.
Transition: aggregate solver score must therefore be read together with PRC.
```

## 7. 五分钟答辩准备

五分钟通常只能回答 3–5 个问题。下面的问题最可能出现。

### Q1. Why use papers from 2026?

> We use recent papers to reduce, although not eliminate, the risk that exact proofs were included in model training. More importantly, the benchmark targets contemporary ML-theory research rather than established textbook results. We still treat contamination as a limitation and plan cross-model and time-based comparisons.

### Q2. How do you prevent leakage?

> The solver receives only an anonymized public task and a shared solver prompt. It cannot access paper metadata, source text, the reference proof, the rubric, the network, retrieval tools, or other agent tools. The runner records the exact exposed files and their hashes.

### Q3. Why one task per paper instead of every theorem?

> Individual lemmas and intermediate theorems can be routine or meaningful only inside a larger proof. Selecting one flagship theorem chain better represents the paper's central theoretical contribution and also prevents multiple highly correlated tasks from the same paper from dominating the benchmark.

### Q4. How reliable is the automatically generated ground truth?

> We separate source recovery, formalization, artifact audit, reference-blind proof review, and GT-assisted evaluation. Downstream objections can trigger bounded revision. The system also records repairable formalization defects separately from solver errors and source-level issues. However, representative human audits are still necessary before release.

### Q5. Why is the stopped or issue rate so high?

> The current labels come from a deliberately conservative automated audit. An issue may occur in an unselected theorem while the final task remains usable, and a stopped case may reflect unresolved validation rather than a confirmed publication error. We therefore report pipeline state and paper-quality mapping separately and will validate these rates on stratified samples.

### Q6. Is the solver actually an agent?

> In the present baseline, the solver is intentionally a single-turn language-model call with no tools, network, or retrieval. Agentic components are used for data construction and evaluation. This gives us a controlled closed-book baseline; agent solvers can be compared later under explicitly defined tool settings.

### Q7. How is PRC different from empirical difficulty?

> Empirical difficulty is observed from solver success rates. PRC is assigned before solution and estimates reconstruction complexity from the proof's scale, dependencies, technical burden, and conceptual structure. The monotonic score trend is evidence that PRC is informative, but it should be tested across additional solver models.

### Q8. Could the evaluator be biased by the reference proof?

> That is why the first reviewer is reference-blind: it checks whether the solver proof is self-contained and mathematically valid without seeing the ground truth. The final evaluator then uses the reference proof and rubric for partial credit and error attribution, while still allowing correct alternative proof routes.

### Q9. What is the main scientific result so far?

> The strongest current result is that the pre-solution PRC levels sharply separate solver scores, from a mean of 9.91 at PRC0 to 4.19 at PRC4. The larger contribution is the auditable pipeline that makes this comparison possible at paper scale. Final claims will be updated after the full run and human calibration.

### Q10. What would you improve with more time?

> I would complete stratified human audits, run multiple solver and evaluator models, quantify inter-evaluator agreement, and test agentic solvers under controlled tool and retrieval settings. I would also publish a de-identified benchmark package with reproducible manifests and evaluation scripts.
